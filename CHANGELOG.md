# Changelog

All notable changes to BSP-Core. Format follows Keep a Changelog; versions follow SemVer.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Upgrade tree now shows the new level immediately after buying; the held totem is re-sent to the client because the upgrade menu has no slots of its own.

### Added
- Shatter Coin item (no recipe yet; operators can give it).
- Placed-only upgrades bought with Shatter Coins from the placed totem's panel: **Fortify** (non-owner mining in a 1/3/5/7/15 block radius is slowed and explosions there spare blocks with rising probability) and **Healing Aura** (heals the owner while they stand within 3/5/7/10/15 blocks). Costs, radii and strengths are all in the `upgrades` config section.
- Translucent glowing spheres show each aura's radius; upgrade orbs around the base are now drawn by a block-entity renderer (five orbs, one per upgrade) and bob gently.
- The placed totem faces the player who placed it; the item shows its face and orb in the inventory and in hand.
- Logout protection: carried totems are placed next to the player when they log out (or at their last main-dimension spot). The pause menu warns before quitting with a totem.
- Completed steals set off a harmless particle and sound blast at the totem and give the thief `steal.invincibilitySeconds` (15) of Resistance V and Fire Resistance.
- Steal countdown now has a shrinking time bar on the HUD and in the totem panel. Below `steal.warningSeconds` (30) the timer and bar pulse red.
- Totem body shades from its state colour at the head down to the plinth colour at the base, in every state and on the item.
- Guardian artwork: a kneeling creeper-man statue holding an ender-pearl orb, as a 3D block model used by both the item and the block. Item pulses turquoise to blue. Block body is gold when owned, turquoise when unclaimed, flashing gold and red while being stolen (`glow` block state). Upgrade levels show as small tinted orbs around the base (`damage`, `resistance`, `mining_speed` block states, 0 to 5), pale at level 1 and vivid at level 5. Assets are generated by `tools/gen_totem_concepts.py assets guardian`.
- Operator column in the totem panel (permission level 2): assign an owner by name (online, cached, or any name for testing), unclaim, cancel or finish the current steal, view and adjust each buff level, reset buffs. Lets one operator test the full steal flow alone.
- Unclaimed placed totems must be stolen like any other, but only take `steal.unclaimedStealSeconds` (60) to claim. Nobody but an operator can mine an unclaimed totem.
- Stealing: right-click a placed totem to open its panel. Non-owners get a **Steal** button. The thief must stay within `steal.radiusBlocks` (5) for `steal.stealSeconds` (300); leaving the radius starts a `steal.graceSeconds` (15) countdown after which the attempt fails. Dying or logging out fails it. One thief at a time. Success transfers ownership. Thief and owner both get a HUD countdown and chat messages.
- Upgrade tree: right-click with the totem in the main hand (sneak + right-click a block to place it instead). Damage, Resistance and Mining Speed buffs with five levels each, bought with XP levels from the `upgrades` config. Upgrades are stored on the totem and survive placing, picking up and stealing. Buffs apply while the totem is in the offhand: +1 attack damage per level, Resistance effect at level-1 amplifier, +25% mining speed per level.
- Operator commands: `/bsp totem give <player>`, `/bsp totem reset <player>`, `/bsp totem locate <player>`, `/bsp totem owner`.
- Dropped totems use their own entity: never despawn, immune to all damage, fire and explosions, return from the void, and place themselves as a block after `restrictions.groundSecondsBeforePlace` seconds (default 30). Only the owner can pick one up.
- Only the owner (or an operator) can mine a placed totem.
- The totem can only be placed or dropped in `restrictions.allowedDimensions` (Overworld, Nether, End). Elsewhere it snaps back into the inventory.
- Totems are pulled back out of any container that is not the player's own inventory.
- On death: killed by a player places every carried totem next to the body immediately; any other death drops them as the 30 second ground item. Deaths outside an allowed dimension send the totem to the player's last position in one. Grave mods never see the totem.
- First-join grant: a server with `first_join.grantTotemOnFirstJoin = true` gives each new player one owned Shatter Totem, recorded in both the player's persistent data and a world-level ledger (`data/bsp_core_totems.dat`) so it can never be granted twice.
- Config section `upgrades` with editable XP level costs for the Damage, Resistance and Mining Speed buffs (defaults 5/10/20/35/55).
- Shatter Totem block, block entity and item (`bsp_core:shatter_totem`): one per stack, fire resistant, epic rarity, glint, cannot be damaged as a dropped item, cannot enter shulker boxes or bundles. Block is immune to explosions, pistons and non-player entities, emits light, and returns the exact totem (owner intact) when mined.
- Totem ownership stored in item NBT and the block entity; shown in the tooltip and on right-click.
- "BSP" creative tab.
- Placeholder textures generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` (no dependencies).
- Project skeleton from the Forge 1.20.1 MDK (Forge 47.4.10).
- Server config with `grantTotemOnFirstJoin` (default off).
