# Changelog

All notable changes to BSP-Core. Format follows Keep a Changelog; versions follow SemVer.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Creating a world no longer crashes when AllTheModium is not installed. The mining-dimension ore files referenced its biome directly; they now use an optional biome tag.
- Operator buttons on the totem panel now update the panel immediately. Unclaim and Reset buffs could leave the panel showing the old owner or levels, because an empty update was being dropped before it reached the client.
- Upgrade tree now shows the new level immediately after buying; the held totem is re-sent to the client because the upgrade menu has no slots of its own.

### Changed
- The Totem Compass cooldown upgrade is now paid in a specific Shatter Coin instead of "coin value": 4 Copper, 4 Gold, 4 Diamond, 4 Netherite, then 2, 4 and 8 Illyrium coins for the seven levels (`compass.cooldownUpgradeCoins` and `compass.cooldownUpgradeCoinTiers`). The screen shows the coin and count under a shorter "Shorten cooldown" button, so the text no longer overflows it.
- Totems upgraded before the tier system are reset to Tier I with no upgrades, because the old levels do not fit tiers and paths. This happens once, the first time such a totem is carried or its chunk loads.
- Resistance is now a percentage of damage removed per level (3% each, 24% at level 8) instead of the Resistance potion effect, which made the holder immune at the old top level. Damage adds 0.5 attack damage per level and Mining Speed 10% per level, so their top levels match the old ones.
- The **Tetrium Crucible** and **Combination Forge** are now animated: burner glow, induction coils, a rising melt, progress lamps and filling trays on the crucible; a striking press head, rail lights, the charge fusing into an ingot and upgrade sockets on the forge.
- The **Totem Compass** screen uses the Holo HUD style: a tile per coin tier showing the tracking time it adds, with Start and the cooldown upgrade below.
- `first_join.grantTotemOnFirstJoin` now defaults to **on**, so a single server or single-player world gives new players their Shatter Totem and guide book without any setup. Networks turn it off on every server except the Spawn Hub. Worlds created before this change keep the value already in their config file.
- RF Upgrades Mk I, II, III now need Tetrium Coils, and the Illyrium Forge Upgrade needs an Illyrium Processor.
- **The Shatter Coin Factory is now a multiblock built in slices.** One slice is 1 wide, 2 high and 3 long: Controller, two Factory Frames, Factory Press, Blank Hatch and Power Port, all new blocks used only by the factory. Slices built side by side, facing the same way and owned by the same player, join into one machine of up to 10 with one screen. A complete slice is drawn as the Cascade Mill: three stepped conveyors, a stamping press, machinery under the belts, glass outer walls, and a coin tray at the front; joined slices lose the wall between them and share a drive shaft, roof rail and tray. Each slice presses its own blanks on its own timing.
- Blanks go in through the Blank Hatch (lower back block) and RF through the Power Port (upper back block). **Coins can no longer be piped out**: they are taken from the tray by hand. RF is shared across a joined machine, and every extra cable on another Power Port charges it faster.
- New factory screen ("Floor Plan"): one lane per slice with blank slot, progress bar, tray and Motivator pips, a shared RF gauge with intake, a power switch, and the owner line.
- The per-player limit of 10 now counts slices.

### Removed
- Speed Gears Mk I, II, III and the factory's upgrade slots. **Motivators** replace them.
- Per-face side configuration on the factory.

### Added
- **Optional MySQL storage for networks.** BSP-Core still keeps all its records in the world, so a single server needs no setup. With `storage.mode = "mysql"` it also connects to a MySQL or MariaDB database that you provide (host, port, database, user and password in the config) and creates its own tables there. Shared across the network: one first-join totem per player, the factory slice limit, and a record of every placed totem and factory slice by server. New operator commands: `/bsp storage status`, `/bsp storage test`, `/bsp storage migrate [force]`. Migrating copies a server's local records into the database, merges with what other servers already put there, and leaves the local records untouched as a backup. If the database cannot be reached, the server carries on from its local records and says so in the log. The slice limit is re-checked against the database every time a controller is placed; a slice that turns out to be over the network limit is removed and handed back.
- The MySQL driver is bundled inside the mod jar. `build/libs/bsp-core-<version>.jar` is the one to release; the `-slim` jar is the same mod without the driver.
- **Demo mode for machines (operators only).** Every machine screen has an OPERATOR box with a DEMO switch that only operators can see or use. With it on, the machine plays its full working animation with nothing in it, for showing machines off at a spawn hub: the Tetrium Crucible, Combination Forge, Illyrium Crucible, Illyrium Refinery and the Shatter Coin Factory (the whole joined machine, cycling through the five coins). It changes only what is drawn; nothing is made or used up, and it survives restarts.
- **Totem tiers and a longer upgrade path.** The Shatter Totem now has five tiers, I to V, each with its own coin (Copper, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, Illyrium). Raising the totem a tier costs 4 coins of the tier being left plus XP levels. Sixteen upgrades sit on five paths in three branches: Carried (Mining Speed, Swiftness, Featherfall, Night Sight; Damage, Resistance, Vitality), Base (Fortify, Alarm, Ward, Deadlock; Healing Aura, Sanctuary, Overclock) and Raid (Lockpick, Shroud). Only four are open at Tier I; each next upgrade opens when the one before it reaches level 2 and the totem is at its tier. Every totem tier adds two levels, so Tier I upgrades reach level 10.
- **Prices name the exact coin.** Each level costs a number of one specific Shatter Coin (the coin of the tier the level is bought at) plus XP levels. All of it is in the config under `upgrades.costs`.
- **Upgrade tree screen** ("Tier Rings") for both the held and the placed totem: the totem in the centre, a ring per tier, the paths as spokes, a panel with the current and next effect and the price shown as the coin's icon, and a box for raising the totem's tier. A wallet row shows your XP and how many of each coin you carry. Base upgrades are bought at the placed totem. Operators get an OPERATOR tab that sets levels and the tier and holds the steal and ownership controls.
- **A totem can never be made unstealable.** New `upgrades.limits` config: intruder mining speed near a totem never drops below 20% (`minIntruderMiningSpeed`), and a steal is never shorter than 60 seconds or longer than 600 (`minStealSeconds`, `maxStealSeconds`), whatever upgrades are in play.
- **Redstone control.** A redstone signal pauses a machine: the Tetrium Crucible, Combination Forge, Illyrium Crucible and Illyrium Refinery (signal at the machine or controller block), and each Shatter Coin Factory slice (signal at that slice's controller). The screen shows "PAUSED BY REDSTONE".
- **Shatter Coins as rare loot.** Copper and Gold coins have a 10% chance each in dungeon, mineshaft and stronghold chests; a Diamond coin has a 5% chance in End City and Ancient City chests. Netherite and Illyrium coins are never found. The chances are data files (`data/bsp_core/loot_modifiers`), so a pack can change them.
- **Advancements.** A BSP-Core tab with 20 advancements that walk a player from their Shatter Totem through Tetrium, the Illyrium machines and the coin factory to a Motivator.
- JEI: clicking the progress dial on a machine screen shows that machine's jobs.
- **BSP Field Guide**, a Patchouli guide book (only when Patchouli is installed). Given with the Shatter Totem on first join and craftable from a Book and a Tetrium Nugget. Four chapters: the Shatter Totem, Tetrium and Illyrium, Machines, Shatter Coins, with recipes and a 3D build page for each multiblock.
- **Assembly Guide.** Sneak + right-click an unfinished multiblock controller (empty hand), or hold Shift over any multiblock block in JEI, to open a step-by-step build guide: a 3D view you can turn, the blocks for the current step pulsing, later ones as small ghosts, a parts list that counts what you have placed, and Back / Play / Next. Covers the Illyrium Crucible, the Illyrium Refinery and the factory slice, including joining a second slice and adding Motivators.
- **JEI support** (optional; the mod still runs without JEI). Pages for the Tetrium Crucible, Combination Forge, Illyrium Crucible, Illyrium Refinery, Shatter Coin Factory and crushing by hand, built from the live config values, plus an information page on every multiblock block explaining how the structure goes together.
- **Crafting recipes for every machine block.** All Illyrium Crucible, Illyrium Refinery and Shatter Coin Factory blocks and the Motivator can now be crafted in survival.
- **15 machine components** that those recipes are built from: Slag Brick (smelted Tetrium Slag, which finally has a use), Tetrium Plate, Machine Chassis, Tetrium Coil, Drive Motor, Circuit Substrate, Basic Control Circuit, Crucible / Refinery / Mint Control Circuits, Thermal Lining, Conveyor Belt, Press Die, Resonance Crystal and Illyrium Processor.
- The **Combination Forge** presses one Tetrium Ingot into one Tetrium Plate.
- **Motivator**: a half-block pylon placed on top of a factory slice, up to three per slice. One, two or three cut that slice's press time by 15%, 30% or 50% (`factory.motivatorTimeReduction`). They pulse faster the more a slice has and link to neighbouring Motivators.
- **Port sockets.** Formed multiblocks now show framed, colour-coded sockets where pipes and cables connect: on the Illyrium Crucible an orange lava socket on each lower Lava Pylon block and a yellow RF socket on each upper one, with the pylons centred in their blocks so pipes and cables line up; the lower blocks take only lava and the upper only RF; on the Refinery Pump a blue water socket with a smaller yellow RF one above (yellow stays dim until an RF upgrade is fitted), and an item socket on each Item Hatch that takes the colour of its mode (blue in, orange out, turquoise both, grey off). The PORTS panel lists every port with the same colours, what it accepts and its mode, plus an RF row.
- **Multiblock visuals.** An unformed controller shows ghost blocks for every missing part. A formed Illyrium Crucible shows its vessel, a melt that rises and changes colour by job, a stirring cross, two containment rings, live lava gauges and a progress bar. A formed Illyrium Refinery shows the water level in its tank, a turning agitator and centrifuge ring, a band coloured by the loaded filter, filter-life lamps and a progress bar. Both structures now include an Illyrium Core, and controllers sit on the bottom layer.
- **Crushing by hand**: a pickaxe and a Tetrium Ingot or Dirty Illyrium Ingot on a crafting table. The pickaxe is kept and loses one durability. One in three Tetrium Ingots becomes Tetrium Dust and one in six Dirty Illyrium Ingots becomes Dirty Illyrium Dust; otherwise the ingot gives back three nuggets: Tetrium Nuggets, or Dirty Illyrium Nuggets, nine of which craft back into a Dirty Illyrium Ingot. All four numbers are in the `hand_crushing` config section.
- **RF Upgrades** Mk I, II, III (Flux Coil, Power Cell, Induction Core): fitted to a machine they let it take RF, run 5%, 10% or 30% faster, and use the same share less lava.
- The **Combination Forge** now burns furnace fuel, or runs on RF at twice the speed with an RF upgrade.
- **Lava Pylon, Refinery Pump and Item Hatch** blocks join the multiblocks and pass pipes through to the controller.
- **Totem Compass**: points to your nearest own totem; load Shatter Coins and start tracking to follow the nearest rival totem for the loaded time, followed by a cooldown that upgrades can shorten from 10 minutes to 3.
- **Illyrium progression.** Two ores (Tetrium, Illyrium, each with deepslate variants and an End Stone Illyrium Ore) generating in the Overworld and, when AllTheModium is installed, in its mining dimension. New materials: Tetrium Slag, Nuggets, Ingots and Dust; Dirty Illyrium Ingots and Dust; Pure Illyrium Dust; Illyrium Nuggets and Ingots. Ingots, nuggets and dusts are recolours of the vanilla shapes.
- **Tetrium Crucible** (single block): Tetrium Ore plus furnace fuel gives nuggets and slag in two outputs.
- **Combination Forge** (single block): nine nuggets into one ingot; forges Illyrium only with the Illyrium Forge Upgrade fitted.
- **Illyrium Crucible** and **Illyrium Refinery** (3x3x3 multiblocks built from Illyrium Casing and Illyrium Tank Glass around a controller that checks the structure). The crucible runs on lava and either smelts ore with slag or alloys pure dust with Tetrium dust. The refinery uses water and a filter to purify dirty dust.
- Filters (Iron, Diamond, Netherite, Illyrium) lasting 1, 20, 100 and 1000 refinements.
- All four machines have per-face item input and output settings; the multiblocks also accept their fluid by pipe.
- Crushing recipes for Mekanism, Create and Thermal turn the ingots into their dusts.
- Shatter Blanks are now made from Tetrium Ingots and each coin tier's blank is built on the previous one. Etherium is renamed Illyrium throughout.
- Five Shatter Coin tiers (Copper, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, Illyrium), each worth double the last, with matching coin blanks. Blank recipes are provisional and cumulative: each blank is crafted around the previous tier's blank.
- Shatter Coin Factory: a craftable-later machine (no recipe yet) that presses blanks into coins in real time (12 h, 24 h, 48 h, 4 d, 7 d by default), catching up when its chunk reloads or the server restarts. Needs Forge Energy (RF), taken per coin when a press starts. Four upgrade slots take Speed Gears Mk I / II / III (5%, 15%, 30% less time, additive, capped by `factory.maxTotalReduction`). Each face can be set to Off, In, Out or In+Out for hoppers, pipes and storage buses. Animated press head, coin on the die, and lit sockets for fitted gears. A player may own at most `factory.maxPerPlayer` (10).
- Illyrium Shard item, the material for Illyrium coin blanks (how it is obtained comes later).
- Totem upgrade costs are now in coin value and can be paid with any mix of coins; change is returned.
- Placed-only upgrades bought with Shatter Coins from the placed totem's panel: **Fortify** (non-owner mining in a 1/3/5/7/15 block radius is slowed and explosions from any source, the owner's included, spare blocks there with rising probability) and **Healing Aura** (heals the owner while they stand within 3/5/7/10/15 blocks). Costs, radii and strengths are all in the `upgrades` config section.
- Translucent glowing spheres show each aura's radius to viewers within `visuals.auraSphereViewDistance` (32) blocks; upgrade orbs around the base are now drawn by a block-entity renderer (five orbs, one per upgrade) and bob gently.
- The placed totem faces the player who placed it; the item shows its face and orb in the inventory and in hand.
- Logout protection: carried totems are placed next to the player when they log out (or at their last main-dimension spot). The pause menu warns before quitting with a totem.
- Completed steals set off a harmless particle and sound blast at the totem and give the thief `steal.invincibilitySeconds` (15) of Resistance V and Fire Resistance.
- Steal countdown now has a shrinking time bar on the HUD and in the totem panel. Below `steal.warningSeconds` (30) the timer and bar pulse red.
- Totem body shades from its state colour at the head down to the plinth colour at the base, in every state and on the item.
- Guardian artwork: a kneeling creeper-man statue holding an ender-pearl orb, as a 3D block model used by both the item and the block. Item pulses turquoise to blue. Block body is gold when owned, turquoise when unclaimed, flashing gold and red while being stolen (`glow` block state). Upgrade levels show as small tinted orbs around the base (`damage`, `resistance`, `mining_speed` block states, 0 to 5), pale at level 1 and vivid at level 5. Assets are generated by `tools/gen_totem_concepts.py assets guardian`.
- Operator column in the totem panel (permission level 2): assign an owner by name (online, cached, or any name for testing), unclaim, cancel or finish the current steal, view and adjust each buff level, reset buffs. Lets one operator test the full steal flow alone.
- Unclaimed placed totems must be stolen like any other, but only take `steal.unclaimedStealSeconds` (60) to claim. Nobody but an operator can mine an unclaimed totem.
- Stealing: right-click a placed totem to open its panel. Non-owners get a **Steal** button. The thief must stay within `steal.radiusBlocks` (5) for `steal.stealSeconds` (300); leaving the radius starts a `steal.graceSeconds` (15) countdown after which the attempt fails. Dying or logging out fails it. One thief at a time. Success transfers ownership. Thief and owner both get a HUD countdown and chat messages.
- Upgrade tree: right-click with the totem in the main hand (sneak + right-click a block to place it instead). Damage, Resistance and Mining Speed buffs with five levels each, bought with XP levels from the `upgrades` config. Upgrades are stored on the totem and survive placing, picking up and stealing. Buffs apply while the totem is in the offhand: +1 attack damage per level, Resistance effect at level-1 amplifier, +25% mining speed per level.
- Operator commands: `/bsp totem give <player>`, `/bsp totem reset <player>`, `/bsp totem locate <player>`, `/bsp totem owner`.
- Dropped totems use their own entity: never despawn, immune to all damage, fire and explosions, return from the void, and place themselves as a block after `restrictions.groundSecondsBeforePlace` seconds (default 30). Only the owner can pick one up.
- Only the owner (or an operator) can mine a placed totem.
- The totem can only be placed or dropped in `restrictions.allowedDimensions` (Overworld, Nether, End). Elsewhere it snaps back into the inventory.
- Totems are pulled back out of any container that is not the player's own inventory.
- On death: killed by a player places every carried totem next to the body immediately; any other death drops them as the 30 second ground item. Deaths outside an allowed dimension send the totem to the player's last position in one. Grave mods never see the totem.
- First-join grant: a server with `first_join.grantTotemOnFirstJoin = true` gives each new player one owned Shatter Totem, recorded in both the player's persistent data and a world-level ledger (`data/bsp_core_totems.dat`) so it can never be granted twice.
- Config section `upgrades` with editable XP level costs for the Damage, Resistance and Mining Speed buffs (defaults 5/10/20/35/55).
- Shatter Totem block, block entity and item (`bsp_core:shatter_totem`): one per stack, fire resistant, epic rarity, glint, cannot be damaged as a dropped item, cannot enter shulker boxes or bundles. Block is immune to explosions, pistons and non-player entities, emits light, and returns the exact totem (owner intact) when mined.
- Totem ownership stored in item NBT and the block entity; shown in the tooltip and on right-click.
- "BSP" creative tab.
- Placeholder textures generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` (no dependencies).
- Project skeleton from the Forge 1.20.1 MDK (Forge 47.4.10).
- Server config with `grantTotemOnFirstJoin` (default off).
