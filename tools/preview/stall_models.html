Market Stall Models

The sixteen block models for the Market Stall (eight skins, normal and broken), rendered from the exact JSON and PNG files in tools/stall/ (Minecraft face shading, cutout, nearest-pixel textures, animated textures running). Each skin is its own build. The front faces north, towards you at the start; drag to turn. The lamps and the floating item are what the mod draws on top, at the points in NOTES.md. Each skin card also shows its Stall Display, one block to the stall's right.

1. The eight skins, each with its Stall Display

2. In a row

Eight stalls side by side on one-block spacing, as they would stand in a market street. Nothing reaches sideways into the next block.

3. Inventory icon

The normal vanilla stall in a slot, using its display.gui transform (rotation 30, 225, 0; scale 0.4; shifted down 3). Worth a check in game.
