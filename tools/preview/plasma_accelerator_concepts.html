Plasma Accelerator Looks

Two machines that carry Wave Plasma from one world to another on the same server. The Plasma Accelerator is a ring, 3 high, 3 wide, 1 deep, with a black hole floating in the hole inside a slowly turning dodecahedron; plasma goes in at the bottom middle block. The Plasma De-accelerator is two blocks long: a core holding a smaller black hole in containment arms, and a port behind it the plasma leaves through. One accelerator links to up to three de-accelerators with Link Cards, both ends burn RF, the flow is one way and keeps every power. Three looks below show the family together, the same way each time: the ring with the de-accelerator beside it, a cable stub where plasma comes in and goes out. Drag a view to turn it.

Unticked: formed but idle, the black hole stays, the glow and the disc go.

4. Decisions, with defaults

  1. Look. A, B or C. Default A, Containment Ring, since the accelerator stands in the plasma room next to extractors, interfaces and tanks.
  2. Screen. S1, S2 or S3. Default S1, Ledger: it reads like the Interface and Tank screens.
  3. Names. "Plasma Accelerator" and "Plasma Decelerator" (one spelling), blocks Accelerator Intake, Accelerator Frame, Decelerator Core, Decelerator Port; items Link Card, Link Expander.
  4. Range. Any distance, same world or another world on this server. Alternative: other worlds only.
  5. Far side asleep (unloaded, no RF, broken): its share goes to the other links; once a minute the accelerator loads the far chunk for a moment and delivers, so a far projector with Anchor relights by itself. WAKE NOW in the screen does it at once.
  6. Splitting. Equal shares between the links that can take plasma this second, the cable rule. Alternative: strict thirds.
  7. Loss. None per hop, no power level lost; RF is the price. Alternative: a tenth, like a repeater.
  8. RF. 200 RF/t at the accelerator while any link delivers, 50 RF/t at a de-accelerator while receiving, 200,000 RF pools, in through any Frame block and any free face of the Core.
  9. Throughput. Up to 1,000 mB/t per feeding run (a face's worth), several runs may feed one intake, 3,000 mB/t in all.
  10. Who may link. Writing a card at an intake needs Machines access on the feeding totem (the valve rule), anyone while nothing has fed it yet; anyone holding a written card may plug it into a core.
  11. Powers. All of them cross, as a tank mixes them, Anchor and the access list included.
  12. Chaining. A de-accelerator's port may feed another accelerator; each hop pays RF.
  13. Recipes. End game: Illyrium ingots and processors, Charged Resonance Crystals, Charged Illyrium cable, a Nether Star in the intake, Eyes of Ender in the core and the expander, a Link Card from paper, illyrium nuggets, an ender pearl and a control circuit. Alternative: Eye of Ender instead of the Nether Star.
  14. Orientation. The ring stands upright facing the player who placed the intake; the de-accelerator lies flat, core in front, port behind.
