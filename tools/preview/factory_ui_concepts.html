Factory Screen Concepts

Three layouts for the Shatter Coin Factory screen when slices are joined. Every slice has its own blank slot, press and coin tray. RF, the power switch and the owner line are shared. The sample data is made up, and each press starts on its own time, as you asked.

A. Line Board

One row per slice, all on screen at once in two columns. Each row reads left to right like the machine: blank slot, progress with time left, coin tray, with Motivator pips and the state above the bar. Best for seeing everything at a glance; rows get busy at ten slices.

B. Selector and Detail

A strip of slice tiles across the top, each with its coin and a small progress bar. Click one to open it below in the same pipeline layout as the other machines: blank, dial, tray. Cleanest and most like the existing screens; you see slots for one slice at a time.

C. Floor Plan

The machine seen from above. Each lane is a slice in the same left-to-right order as the blocks: blank slot at the back, a belt that fills toward the front as the coin is pressed, tray at the front. Easiest to match a lane to the real slice; text per lane is short.
