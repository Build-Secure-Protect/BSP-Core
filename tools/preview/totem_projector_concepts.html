Totem Projector Concepts

Three connected features: a Totem Generator that sits under a Shatter Totem and draws off some of its powers, a Totem Projector that recreates them as a second aura somewhere else, and the Totem Cable that joins the two. Three looks for each block, three cable styles and three screens. The totem shown is the real in-game model. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

1. Totem Generator

Goes directly under a placed Shatter Totem. Needs RF.

2. Totem Projector

Placed anywhere a cable can reach. Makes an aura bubble like the totem's own. Needs RF.

3. Totem Cable

Four cables, each with a longest run it can carry the totem's powers over. Pick a cable to see each style in its colour.

4. The Generator screen

Where the owner chooses which of the totem's powers to send, and sees how much arrives. The made-up totem here is Tier III with Fortify 4, Healing Aura 3, Ward 2, Alarm 1 and Overclock 2; the cable run is set with the slider.

A. Power List

A plain list of the totem's base powers with a switch each, and what level each arrives at. Simple; little sense of the cable.

B. Channel Slots

The generator has a fixed number of channels (two here). Click a channel to choose which power it carries. Makes "a set amount of the powers" very clear, and channels could be added by an upgrade later.

C. Signal Path

The whole chain drawn left to right: totem, generator, cable, projector, with the numbers at each stage and the powers underneath. Shows at a glance where strength is lost.

How it would work

What can be sent

  1. Only the totem's base powers, the ones that work around a placed totem: Fortify, Healing Aura, Alarm, Ward, Sanctuary, Overclock.
  2. Not Deadlock (it is about stealing that totem) and not carried or raid powers.
  3. A generator sends two powers at once.

Strength and distance

  1. Full strength next to the generator, falling in a straight line to half at the cable's longest run. Levels round down, never below 1.
  2. Past the cable's limit nothing arrives.
  3. Cable types can be mixed; the shortest-reach type in the run sets the limit.

Rules

  1. The projector works for the totem's owner, like the totem's own aura. If the totem is stolen, it now works for the thief.
  2. One projector per generator.
  3. Both blocks need RF all the time. No power, no aura.
  4. The projector is not a totem: it cannot be stolen and scores nothing.
