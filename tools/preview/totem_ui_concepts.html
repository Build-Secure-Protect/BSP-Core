Totem Upgrade System Concepts

Two things to decide here. First, which new power-ups the Shatter Totem should get. Second, how one screen should present all of them. The three mock-ups below are working: click upgrades and buy levels with the sample XP and coins. The numbers are placeholders.

Proposed upgrade catalogue

Sixteen upgrades in three branches. Five exist today; eleven are new ideas marked NEW. Carried upgrades work while you hold the totem and cost XP. Base upgrades only work while the totem is placed and cost Shatter Coins. Raid upgrades help you take other totems.

A. Constellation

A skill tree. The totem sits in the centre and each branch fans out from it; a node fills in as you level it. Click a node to see what it does and buy the next level. Feels the most like a game progression screen, and shows the whole system at a glance, but node labels are short and it needs the side panel for detail.

B. Console

The Holo HUD of the machines, with tabs: Overview, Carried, Base, Raid. Each upgrade is a row with its level, what it gives now and next, and a buy button. The plainest and the easiest to extend: a new upgrade is one more row, a new branch is one more tab.

C. Loadout

Adds a choice. You still buy levels, but the totem only has a few sockets (three carried, three base, one raid), so you pick which upgrades are active. Click a card to select it, then slot or unslot it. This makes builds and counters part of PvP, at the cost of being a new rule to learn and balance.
