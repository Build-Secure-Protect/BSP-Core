Illyrium Progression Blueprint

The proposed BSP-Core resource chain from two new ores to the fully upgraded Shatter Totem. Second pass: vanilla-shaped ingots, nuggets and dusts, more detailed futuristic machines, the two Illyrium machines as multiblock structures, RF upgrade options and the Totem Compass. Nothing on this page is in the mod yet.

Progression flow

Read each lane left to right. Lanes feed each other: a chip under a step shows what it takes from, or sends to, another lane.

Machines

Animated cuboid mock-ups at Minecraft pixel scale. Drag to rotate. The two Tetrium-stage machines are single blocks. The two machines used only for Illyrium are 3×3 multiblock structures; faint lines on the floor mark the block grid.

Ores and items

16×16 sprites. Ingots follow the standard iron ingot shape, nuggets the iron nugget, and dusts the redstone pile, recoloured: violet for Tetrium, turquoise for Illyrium, murky green with rust flecks for the dirty stage.

Proposed recipes

Filters and the forge upgrade follow your notes exactly. Everything else is a suggestion that only uses things available at that point in the chain. The three RF upgrades are alternatives to choose between, or tiers if you want all three.

Totem Compass

Crafted from Tetrium and Illyrium. It always points to the nearest totem you own. Feeding it a Shatter Coin makes it track other players' totems for a short time.

Three balance levels

What one Illyrium Ingot costs end to end, computed from the per-step numbers in each column. Standard is the chosen starting point.

Time is machine time for one of each machine running the whole chain in series, including the Tetrium that feeds it. Filter uses equal the pure dust refined: one Iron Filter per dust, a Diamond Filter lasts 20, Netherite 100, Illyrium 1000. The first seven Illyrium Ingots must be assembled by hand on a crafting table, because the Combination Forge needs them for its Illyrium upgrade.
