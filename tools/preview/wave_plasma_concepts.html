Wave Plasma Concepts

Round 2 of the totem energy rework, with your picks applied. The energy is now Wave Plasma, a fluid the totem gives off: it is pumped, piped and stored like a liquid, measured in mB. Blocks: Plasma Extractor, Plasma Interface, Plasma Repeater, Plasma Cable, Wave Emitter, and later Plasma Tanks. Drag any 3D view to turn it.

6. Rules and numbers (proposed, now in plasma)

Totem output

A placed totem gives off 100 mB/t of Wave Plasma at Tier I. The new Base upgrade Output (sixth path, next to Anchor) raises it to 150, 200, 300, 400, 500 mB/t over six levels. Extractors split it evenly. A projector needs 100 mB/t arriving to project, and no longer uses RF. An extractor holds 4,000 mB; a projector 2,000 mB.

Pressure

A cable run carries its full length at full pressure, so the fall-off along the cable goes. Each repeater pumps the plasma into a fresh run but loses pressure doing it: 90% of the flow passes each repeater. Powers lose levels as before.

So a projector behind repeaters needs the Output upgrade or a second totem on the interface to reach 100 mB/t. The STATUS tab shows the pressure arriving so the player can see why a projector is short.

Interface limit

Unchanged: up to 12 joined blocks; a thirteenth, or an interface touching an extractor another interface already routes, is refused and shows a red seam. Twelve blocks is its longest reach.

What a projector receives

Everything on its interface: the powers of every totem on an extractor it touches, and (round 3) the powers held in Plasma Tanks on extractors. A projector receives two powers at once, three with a Channel Expander fitted in it.

Plasma as a fluid

Wave Plasma is a real Forge fluid, so later a Plasma Tank block can store it, pipes from other mods could move it if you ever want that (off by default), and batteries are tanks: a charged battery is a tank of plasma that also remembers which powers were in the totem it was filled from.
