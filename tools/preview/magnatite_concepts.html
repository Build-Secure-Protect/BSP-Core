Magnatite And Centrifuge Concepts

A new metal and the machine that processes it. Magnatite sits between Tetrium and Illyrium: slower to make than Tetrium, far quicker than Illyrium, and its charged form is what Decoy Totems and later machines are built from. Three looks for the ore and items, three for the Magnetic Centrifuge. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

1. Magnatite ore and items

Each row shows the ore in stone and in deepslate, then the nugget, ingot, Charged Magnatite Ingot and Carbon Dust in that colour scheme, drawn at game size.

2. The Magnetic Centrifuge

A multiblock one block high, three wide and three deep. More centrifuges can be stacked on top, up to six. Use the controls to stack them, run them, and fit the Copper Tetrium Coil that lets them charge ingots.

3. How Magnatite is made, and how long it takes

These times assume a failed try gives the plain ingot back to try again, so the cost of charging is time and power, not lost metal. Stacking mostly speeds up the charging step; the three minutes to make the ingot stay unless the stack also separates ore faster.

Proposed blocks (9 per layer)

  1. Centrifuge Controller: front centre.
  2. Centrifuge Rotor: the middle block.
  3. Centrifuge Casing x 5, Item Hatch x 1 (the existing one), Centrifuge Power Port x 1.
  4. A stacked layer needs a Rotor and 8 Casing only; the bottom layer has the controller and ports.

Proposed new items

  1. Magnatite Nugget, Magnatite Ingot, Charged Magnatite Ingot.
  2. Carbon Dust: by-product; used for the Rotor and the coil's insulation, so it is not waste.
  3. Copper Tetrium Coil: the upgrade that turns on charging. Tetrium Coils wound with copper around a Magnatite Ingot.

Proposed cost

  1. Casing: Tetrium Plates and a Machine Chassis.
  2. Rotor: a Drive Motor, Dirty Illyrium Ingots and Carbon Dust, so the Illyrium Crucible has to come first.
  3. Needs RF to run, more when charging.
  4. The Magnet Core for a decoy would need several Charged Magnatite Ingots and an Illyrium Processor.
