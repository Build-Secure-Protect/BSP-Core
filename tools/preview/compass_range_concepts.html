Compass Range Concepts

Three ways to fit the two new range ladders onto the Totem Compass screen, and three ways to show a compass's upgrades on its hotbar icon. They follow your answers. Range limits rival tracking only, your own totems are found at any distance, the horizontal range is a circle, and loaded time keeps running when no rival is in range.

The example compass has Across level 2 (500 blocks), Up or down level 1 (20 blocks) and cooldown level 3 (7:00), with 2:30 of tracking time loaded. Coin pictures are stand-ins; the game draws the real ones.

1. The compass screen

Today the screen is 236 by 158: five coin tiles load tracking time, then START and "Shorten cooldown" side by side, with the cooldown on the last line. Each concept adds Across and Up or down.

A. One upgrade table

START gets the full width. Under it, an UPGRADES table puts the cooldown and both ranges in the same row shape: the name, what it is now, a pip per level, and a key that shows the price of the next level. The screen grows 20 pixels taller. Hovering over a row's key shows what the next level gives.

B. TRACK and UPGRADE tabs

The screen keeps its size and gains two tabs. TRACK loads coins and starts, and says the compass's reach beside START; while tracking with nothing in range it says so in red. UPGRADE shows the three ladders as columns of every value: bought ones in white, the current one lit, the next one with its price on the key.

C. A reach picture

The top half stays as it is today. A REACH panel underneath draws the compass's reach from the side: your position in the middle, the turquoise box as far across and as far up and down as it finds rivals, and the next level as a gold outline. WIDER and DEEPER keys with their prices sit beside it.

The tooltip (all three)

The compass's tooltip gains a reach line, so a player sees what a compass can do before opening it, and when deciding which compass to keep.

2. The hotbar icon

Each picture is a hotbar holding six compasses, from a new one (0 across / 0 up or down) to a fully upgraded one (6 / 6), with the same six at twice the size underneath. The needle still turns as it does today.

A. The rim changes metal

The rim takes the colour of the coin that paid for the upgrades so far, counting both ladders together (0 to 12 levels): Tetrium purple at 0, then copper, gold, diamond, netherite, and Illyrium turquoise at 12. One look says roughly how strong a compass is, but not which ladder.

B. Pips along two edges

Turquoise pips along the bottom count the Across levels; gold pips up the right side count the Up or down levels. They are drawn in inventory slots only, like a durability bar, not on the compass in your hand. Exact, but tiny at normal GUI scale.

C. Two gems in the rim

A gem sits in the rim at the top for Across and one at the bottom for Up or down. Each gem takes the colour of the coin that paid for its ladder's last level: none, copper, gold, diamond, netherite, then Illyrium for levels 5 and 6, with a white glint at 6.

How it would be built: A and C keep the 32 needle pictures and add a rim or gem layer coloured from the compass's levels in code, so no new picture files per level. B draws the pips over the item in a slot, the way Minecraft draws a durability bar.
