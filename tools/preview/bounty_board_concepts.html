Bounty Board

Players put a price on another player's head, in pieces or Shatter Coins. Hunters accept bounties at the board. When a hunter who accepted kills the target, they collect the reward, plus a bonus if the kill comes in time. Four things to pick: the block, the player screens, the Score Screen board and the admin tab. The examples run on live timers, so the countdowns tick down.

1. The Bounty Board block

An old western board with digital wanted posters. Admins place it, like the Trade Post. Drag a view to turn it. Each poster screen shows a live bounty and changes every few seconds. The labelled keys open the screen on that page.

A. Frontier Notice Board

2 wide × 2 tall, the Trade Post's footprint. Two square posts with rope lashings hold a pitched shingle roof and a backboard of six uneven planks. A carved WANTED sign sits under the roof. Four poster screens in brass bezels are nailed on, each a little crooked, with torn paper notices on the posts. A lantern hangs from the eave, and a shelf at the bottom carries the BOARD, PLACE and HUNTS keys.

B. Sheriff's Kiosk

3 wide × 2 tall, on a plank deck. A tall middle panel holds one big screen with the sheriff's star above it. Two lower wings hold two small screens each, under their own tin awnings. A slanted desk carries the keys and a brass coin slot. A hitching rail and a water barrel stand in front.

C. Wanted Door

2 wide × 3 tall, a braced plank door on two feet. At the top, a round clock counts down the soonest bounty to end. Below it, three landscape posters hang crooked with curled corners. A revolver cylinder at the side turns the pages, and a scrolling ticker runs above the keys. A knife is stuck in the wood and a rope coil hangs on a peg.

2. The player screens

Each layout shows browsing, a bounty's page with ACCEPT, and placing a bounty. Every screen has the same four parts: the player, the reward in pieces or coins, notes for hunters only, and the time with an optional bonus. Use the switch to see a bounty as a hunter who has accepted it. Only then do the notes show.

Notes stay hidden until you accept.

A. Poster Wall

Browse shows six posters at a time, three across and two down, under the WANTED, MY HUNTS and MY BOUNTIES tabs, with a search box and a sort key. PLACE A BOUNTY stands apart at the top right. Click a poster to open its own page: a big poster on the left, and on the right the end time, the bonus, the hunter count, the notes, and ACCEPT. Placing puts four numbered blocks on one page, with the total you pay above PLACE BOUNTY.

B. Ledger and Detail

A list on the left gives one row per bounty: head, name, reward, time left and hunters. The right pane always shows the selected bounty, with its notes and ACCEPT, so there is no separate page. Placing has the player list down the left and the other three parts on the right. This example pays in coins, so the coin tier chips and a count show.

C. Sheriff's Desk

All keys sit in one column on the right, like the bank screens: pages at the top, ACCEPT and PLACE A BOUNTY at the bottom. You flick through one big poster at a time with the arrows, and a strip of heads underneath shows where you are. Placing is a four-step wizard. Step 4 is a 30-day timeline: the flag marks the end, and the gold band marks the bonus window.

3. The Score Screen board

Admins pick the board in a Score Screen's settings, alone or in the rotation. Each target gets one line: their head, the total on their head, the time left on the bounty that ends soonest, and a BONUS mark while a bonus is still on offer. Calamity_Jo has two bounties, added together. Looks A and B already exist on the Score Screen. Look C is a new look.

A. Wanted Ledger existing Ledger look

One row per target: the head, the name, a grey line with the time left and the hunter count, and the reward on the right. Long boards scroll at the screen's speed. This needs no change to the Score Screen.

B. Poster Tiles existing Tiles look

Tiles with the head on top, the reward in large type, then the name, and the time left under that. More posters fit across on a wider screen.

C. Most Wanted new look

The biggest bounty gets a large poster with a countdown to the second and a bar for its bonus window, and the next five sit in a list on the right. This is a new look in the Score Screen settings, so it is more work, and other mods' boards could use it too.

4. The admin tab

Staff can add a bounty, remove one with or without a refund, extend its time, see who placed it, and read or blank its notes. The panel already has seven tabs at 37 pixels, so an eighth needs room.

A. Eight narrow tabs

All eight tabs shrink to 33 pixels, and the ADMIN heading shrinks to a violet diamond. The labels get small: RADIATION is the tightest. The body shows the bounty list on the left and the selected bounty on the right: who placed it, the hunters, and the notes. Along the bottom sit EXTEND +1 H, +1 D and +7 D, then REMOVE (with a refund), then REMOVE, NO REFUND, with a gap between each group.

B. Two rows of tabs

The tabs go into two rows of four at full width with readable labels, and the panel grows 14 pixels taller. The body is a table. Clicking a row opens a strip of actions under it.

C. A BOUNTIES key on the PLAYERS tab

No new tab. A BOUNTIES key beside BANK & MARKET opens a wide page about the selected player, like the 1.18.0 bank page. Its switches show bounties on them, placed by them, or being hunted by them, or ALL BOUNTIES, and ADD BOUNTY ON THIS PLAYER sits at the top.
