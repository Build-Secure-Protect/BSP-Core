Team Screen Concepts

Three looks for the Team screen, mock-up set 1 of the Teams line. The same five views in each: no team yet (name, colour, CREATE), the team (members with where they are, the leader marked), invite (players online and not in a team), messages (the pinned note, then the last fifty lines, new ones marked, a line to type) and settings (leader only: rename, recolour, hand over, remove, disband). Every look opens the same three ways: the TEAM tab on a placed totem's panel, the TEAM key on the held totem's screen, and a key binding. Toggle who is looking: the leader sees every key, a member sees no settings.

View: Looking:

Dots: green = online here, amber = online on another server of the network, grey = offline with the last time seen. The crown marks the leader. Team names show in the team's chat colour.

1. The three looks

2. Rules every look carries

  1. Keys grouped by job with a gap (the owner's UI rule): the team's own actions (INVITE, TEAM CHAT) apart from the leaving ones (LEAVE, DISBAND), and settings apart from both.
  2. Two confirms for LEAVE, REMOVE, HAND OVER and DISBAND, drawn in the key itself ("LEAVE?" then "SURE?"), no popup window.
  3. TEAM CHAT is a toggle key: lit while the player's chat goes to the team; the HUD badge (mock-up set 2) shows the same state.
  4. Invite lists players online on this server who are in no team, with one INVITE key per row; a sent invite shows "waiting 54 s" in the row until answered or expired.
  5. Messages read like chat: oldest at the top, newest at the bottom, the pinned note above the list, new lines marked; the input line sends to the team without leaving the screen.
  6. Every string goes through the CashMachine fit and wrap helpers; names are cut with an ellipsis, never overflow.
  7. The leader cannot leave while others remain (the LEAVE key says HAND OVER FIRST); a member sees no settings view at all.

3. Decisions for this round

  1. Look: A the panel tab (team life lives inside the totem's panel, no new window), B the Cash Machine screen (the bank's skin, cream keys, phosphor text), or C the roster board (member cards and the message board side by side, the panel's palette with the operator's violet). Default: A, with the key binding and the held totem's TEAM key opening the same panel without a totem.
  2. Members shown as: rows (A, B) or cards with heads (C)? A card could also carry "2 totems" and the last seen time. Default: rows.
  3. Message board: its own view (A, B) or always on screen beside the members (C)? Default: own view, with a "3 new" badge on the MESSAGES key.
  4. Colour picker: the 16 chat colours as swatches (shown) or a cycling key? Default: swatches.
  5. Create without a totem placed: the key binding and the held screen open the Team screen alone, so a new player can make or join a team before placing. Default: yes.
  6. Chat toggle on the screen: keep TEAM CHAT as a key here (shown) or only on the HUD badge and /t? Default: key here too.
