Shop On/Off Switch

Where the shop Workbench turns a shop on and off (BSP-Server Utilities request SU-BC8). An off shop never opens for players and its stalls go dark; staff still edit it and open it with Test shop. Only a top shop has the switch: a section follows its top shop. Each look is drawn at the Workbench's real size with the Spring Sale shop open. Use the buttons to see the shop on, off, or a section of it, and the Settings screen or the shop list.

Shop
Screen

A. A lit key in the key column

A key of its own sits in the middle of the key column on every screen, in its own group with a gap above and below. It glows green and reads Shop is on while players can open the shop; it turns plain and reads Shop is off when it is off. Click it to switch. For a section it is locked and reads Follows top. The heading adds a red OFF tag while the shop is off, and the shop list gets an On column.

B. The first line of Settings

The Settings screen opens with a group of its own: Open to players ... on in green, or off in red. It toggles with a click like every other on/off line. A section shows follows Spring Sale and does not toggle. The shop list gets an On column (yes, a red off, or a dash for a section). Nothing changes on the other screens.

C. A strip across the top of the screen

While the shop is off, a red strip under the heading reads CLOSED FOR CHANGES, with a small Open it button at its right. It shows on every screen (page, settings, style), so nobody forgets a shop left off. While on, a thin green strip reads OPEN TO PLAYERS, with a Close it button. A section's strip names its top shop, and its button is locked. In the shop list each name starts with a lamp: green on, red off, hollow for a section.
