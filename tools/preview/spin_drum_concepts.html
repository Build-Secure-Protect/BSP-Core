Spin Drum Detail Concepts

The Magnetic Centrifuge you picked (the Spin Drum), reworked with much more detail and far less empty space. Three treatments of the same machine. Each layer is one block high and three by three; stack up to six. Drag to turn. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

The same in all three

The bottom layer carries the controller screen on the front, the Item Hatch (blue socket) on one side and the Power Port (yellow socket) on the other. Stacked drums turn opposite ways. With the coil fitted, the copper parts glow blue while it charges. Status lamps on each layer show green when running.
