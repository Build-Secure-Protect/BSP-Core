Plasma Tank Mob Chamber

A tank fitted as a chamber spawns mobs from its biome's lists on its own timer, one per 2×2 of floor, and only while it is at least 15% full. The mobs stand still with no AI and burn in the plasma. Their loot goes to the tank's loot store, and the drops you choose are fed in to tag plasma with a power that a Projector gives out in its reach. Pick one look from each of the four sets below. Drag any 3D view to turn it.

1. The chamber upgrade

What turns a plain tank into a chamber. Each view is a 6×5×6 tank in the plains: inside 4×3×4, so four mobs at once, up to 3 blocks tall. Mobs appear, burn for a few seconds at 4 health a second, fall, and drop their loot, which slides to the port into the loot store.

55%

2. The CHAMBER tab

A second tab on the tank's screen (380 × 214 GUI pixels, drawn at twice size). It has the fill meter with the "mobs spawn" mark at 15%, the plasma pools as coloured bands, the ALL / HOSTILE / PASSIVE switch, what can spawn here, the loot store, and the power picker. Click the switch, the levels and the powers. The numbers are an example tank: 6×5×6 in the plains, 132 shell blocks, 330,000,000 mB.

61%Drag below 15% to see spawning stop.

A. Two panes

The fill bar runs across the top with a gold notch at 15%. On the left: the switch, the spawn status, then every mob that can spawn here with its chance, its drop and that drop's power. On the right: the 27-slot loot store, and under it the power picker, which shows what the chosen drop will tag in mB and in projector-minutes. Everything is on one page.

B. Gauge column

A tall fill gauge down the left edge, like the tank's lit uprights, with the "mobs spawn" tag sticking out at 15% and the pools stacked as bands from the bottom. The middle holds the spawn table, with columns for mob, chance, drop and power. The right column holds the loot store and the picker. The meter reads the same way as the tank in the world.

C. Three sub-tabs

The fill bar stays on top, and under it three sub-tabs: SPAWNS, LOOT and POWERS, each with room for bigger rows (chance bars, a line per stored drop, a pool legend). It is the least crowded layout, but it takes more clicks to see everything at once.

3. The Tank Extractor

A block against a Tank Port. It sends one pool out to the cables: plain plasma, totem-powered plasma, or one mob power. The cable in each view carries the chosen pool's colour.

4. The Wither Bone

The item a wither skeleton's bones become in a chamber, so its power (Wither on outsiders) is kept apart from Growth. These are 16×16 item sprites, shown at 8× size.
