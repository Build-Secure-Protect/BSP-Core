Decoy Totem Concepts 2

Second round, with your changes. The decoy is the Hollow Idol. To its owner it shows the idol with a ghost of a Shatter Totem around it; to everyone else it looks exactly like a real Shatter Totem. The totem here is the real in-game model and texture. It stands on a dedicated power block that needs 100 RF per tick. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

Without power, or once broken, the disguise drops and everyone sees the bare idol.

1. The ghost the owner sees

Three ways to draw the ghost totem around the idol. Switch "Seen by" to another player and all three become the same ordinary-looking totem.

2. The power block underneath

A dedicated block the decoy must stand on. It takes RF from cables on its sides or bottom and feeds the decoy 100 RF per tick. Shown here with the decoy on top, as its owner sees it.

3. The screen: upgrade items on a tree

The two ideas combined: upgrades are crafted items, and they go into sockets laid out as a tree, so a later socket only opens once the one before it is filled. Click sockets to fit and remove items; click the trap charge again to change its kind.

A. Socket Tree

The magnet core in the middle with three branches growing out of it, like the totem's upgrade tree. Most like what you already know.

B. Bench Rows

Three rows read left to right, one per path, with the numbers beside them. Clearest to read, least like a tree.

C. Core Rings

Sockets on two rings around the core, like the totem's tier rings: the inner ring first, then the outer. Compact, and leaves room for a third ring later.
