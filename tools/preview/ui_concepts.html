Machine Screen Concepts

Three directions for the BSP machine screens, each shown for the Illyrium Crucible mid-job. All three drop the text warning, mark inputs in blue and outputs in orange, show an output arrow, and move port, upgrade and power controls out of the main panel. Blue means input, orange output, grey off, violet an upgrade slot, red something missing.

A. Tabbed Console

Closest to the Mekanism screen you sent. The main panel holds only the process. Tabs hang off the left edge and open a drawer; the status column stays on the right.

  • Left tabs: Ports, Upgrades, Power. The Ports drawer is shown open, listing each designated port by name with its mode.
  • Click a port's chip to cycle input, output, off.
  • Power tab is the on/off switch and redstone behaviour.
  • Right column: missing items in red, plus an info tab.
  • Least change from today; everything has an obvious home.

B. Schematic

The left half is a top-down plan of the actual structure with its ports as coloured nodes, so you configure a port by clicking where it physically is.

  • Ports are the dots on the plan: Item Hatches left and right, Lava Pylons at the corners. Colour shows the mode.
  • The process runs top to bottom on the right, with the arrow pointing at the output.
  • A missing input glows red in its own slot, so no separate column is needed.
  • Upgrades and the power switch sit in a strip along the bottom, always visible.
  • Best for multiblocks; single-block machines would show their six faces instead of a plan.

C. Holo HUD

The most science-fiction of the three. A horizontal pipeline with a progress dial in the middle, segmented gauges, and tabs along the bottom.

  • Dial shows percent and time left; chevrons animate toward the output in game.
  • Lava and RF are horizontal segmented bars with labels.
  • Bottom tabs: Process, Ports, Upgrades, Status. The Ports tab is shown beside the process, using an unfolded-cube face grid like Mekanism's side configuration.
  • Status is a checklist of icons with ticks and crosses.
  • Widest panel; the biggest departure from vanilla screens.
