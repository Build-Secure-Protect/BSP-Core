Totem Forge Concepts

Two pieces of work that meet in one machine. Section 1 gives BSP-Core's metals molten forms that Tinkers, Mekanism, AE2 and the rest can pump and store, without letting any other mod turn ore into more metal than BSP's own machines do. Sections 2 to 5 are the Totem Forge: a 2 by 2 by 3 multiblock that melts a large bill of metals and pours them into an eight-piece mould around a Totem Core, then lets the totem set: 5 days for a player who owns no totem, or 15 days after paying Shatter Coins for a player who already owns one. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

1. Molten metals

Three looks for the three molten metals, animated as they would flow in a tank or a smeltery, with each one's bucket. Only Tetrium, Illyrium and Magnatite get a fluid; Allthemodium and the others the forge takes as ingots.

Same metal in, same metal out

Every number here is 90 mB to an ingot and 10 mB to a nugget, Tinkers' own scale. Ore melts to exactly the nuggets a BSP machine makes from it, in the same time, and the by-products (slag, Carbon Dust) only come from the BSP machine, so the BSP route stays the better one.

What other mods can do with them

  • Tinkers' Construct: melt and cast as in the table. Casting a nugget or an ingot gives back exactly what went in.
  • Mekanism, AE2, Refined Storage, Thermal, Ender IO, Create, Pipez: pump, pipe and store the fluids. None of them gets a recipe that makes or multiplies the metal.
  • Totem Forge: takes the fluids straight from a pipe, so a player can melt in a smeltery and pump in.

What stays closed

  • No item tags such as forge:ingots/tetrium or forge:ores/illyrium. JAOPCA, Almost Unified, Mekanism and Thermal build ore doubling from those, so they stay off as they are today.
  • Only fluid tags (forge:molten_tetrium and so on), which Tinkers needs for casting.
  • The Tinkers recipes are plain melting recipes, not ore melting, so the smeltery's ore bonus and the foundry's by-products never apply.

2. The Totem Forge (look A)

The Hearth and Crucible look, with its working parts. Eight part blocks in two layers of four: the bottom holds the Forge Controller, the Metal Intake and two Forge Hearths, the top two Melt Tanks, the Pour Head and the Forge Cap. The middle layer is the hollow where the mould stands at the totem's real size. Move the slider or press Play to place the eight pieces, pour and let the totem set. Drag the view to turn it.

Where things go in

  • Items (left side): the Metal Intake's hatch, ringed in blue. Ingots from a hopper, a pipe or by hand.
  • Fluids (left side): the flange with the turquoise ring and a red valve wheel. Molten metal from any pipe or tank.
  • Power (right side): the gold socket wound with copper. RF from any cable; it speeds melting like the RF upgrades do on the other machines.
  • Mould pieces: right-click the hollow with a piece in hand; it appears in place.

What you can read from outside

  • Front panel: the screen, three lamps (mould complete, tanks full, owner check) and the red POUR button under a clear flip cover, with a lever beside it to lock the forge.
  • Tanks: a window on the front and the left of the top layer, and glow strips up the front pillars.
  • Pipes: the feed pipe climbs the back left corner from the intake to the tanks, with a pressure dial; the hot line up the back right glows as high as the metal stands.
  • Pour Head: a nozzle under the top layer and a hand wheel on its front. The chimney smokes while the hearths burn.

3. The Totem Mould

Each mould piece is an item drawn like the totem in your hotbar: the whole totem, with the piece it moulds picked out and the rest of the figure left translucent. Three ways to pick it out, each shown in hotbar slots.

4. Recipes

One draft set, built on what the pack already gates late: Illyrium, Charged Magnatite, the Allthemodium metals, Nether Stars and Totems of Undying. Hover a cell to see its name.

5. The forge screen (one draft)

Right-click the controller. The mould slots fill as pieces go into the chamber and each tube is one metal of the Standard bill. When every tube is full, a player with no totem pours free and the totem sets in 5 days; a player who owns one pays Shatter Coins first and it sets in 15 days.
