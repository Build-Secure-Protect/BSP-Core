Lottery Concepts

Three looks for the lottery, each drawn in all the places a player meets it: the Trade Post's new LOTTERY page, the admin page, a Lottery board for the Score Screen and a line on the Portable Banker. The screens are drawn at Minecraft GUI size in the Trade Post's own phosphor colours. Pick one look per place; they can be mixed.

Picked (owner, 2026-10-28)

LOTTERY page B (name drum), admin page C (player table), Score Screen board B (drum), Portable Banker B (a small card). Every shop kind pays the pot, in pieces or coins (kits do not); the admin page sets the days with − and +, starting at 7.

Decided (owner, 2026-10-28)

  • Who is in: everyone who logged on to any server of the network in the week before the draw, one equal chance each. No tickets.
  • Left out: operators, and the LuckPerms ranks the admin ticks on the admin page (mod and staff, say).
  • The draw: only when an admin presses DRAW. The winner takes the whole pot, straight into their bank, online or not.
  • The pot fills from the market's two 5% taxes and half of every shop purchase.
  • Announced by a countdown from 5 in chat, then the winner's name and the amount.
  • Admin page in the Trade Post (operators only) and by /bsp lottery admin.

1. The LOTTERY page on the Trade Post

A fourth tab beside MARKET, SELL and COLLECT. The traders band stays at the right. Pick a moment to see each look then.

A. Jackpot

The pot in big amber digits with a coin on each side, under it how many players are in and your own chance, then the last five winners with the date and what they won. During the countdown the number is replaced by the count; after it, by the winner's name. Operators see an ADMIN key at the bottom.

B. Name drum

A drum of the names of everyone in turns slowly on the left, the middle row marked with amber arrows. The pot, how many are in and your chance sit beside it. The last five winners are ticket stubs along the bottom. During the countdown the drum spins fast and stops on the winner, lit amber.

C. Ledger

Three figures in boxes across the top (pot, players in, your chance), then the list of everyone in with where and when they last played, so a player can check they are on it. The last winner is one line at the foot. Operators get a fifth tab, a cog, for the admin page.

2. The admin page

Operators only: from the Trade Post or by /bsp lottery admin anywhere (then without the traders band). Everything to run the lottery: the pot, who is in, the ranks left out, the week's length, adding or taking pieces with a reason, and the draw.

A. Two columns

The pot and the players in at the left with the week's length (−/+ a day) and the big amber DRAW key; the LuckPerms ranks as tick boxes at the right. DRAW asks once more on a card ("48,210 pieces to one of 62 players") before anything happens.

B. Control panel

Three readouts along the top, the ranks as chips you tap on and off, and at the foot a red DRAW button under a clear cover, like the Totem Forge's pour button: the first press lifts the cover, the second draws.

C. Player table

The ADMIN tab takes the whole screen: every player seen this season in a table, each marked IN or OUT with the reason (a rank, not seen for 9 days, operator). The controls sit in a column at the right, DRAW at its foot with a second press to confirm.

3. The Lottery board on the Score Screen

A new "Lottery" board in the Score Screen's settings, shown alone or in the rotation. Each is a 4 by 2 screen. There is no countdown to a next draw, since the admins choose when.

A. Big pot

The pot across the middle in large gold digits that tick up as it grows, how many are in under it, and the last winner on a strip at the foot.

B. Drum

The same name drum as the page's look B rolling at the left, the pot and the last three winners at the right.

C. Beside the leaderboard

The Side look every board has: the totem leaderboard keeps the left two thirds, the lottery takes a column at the right.

4. The Portable Banker

A line on the account page, which every player has, so the pot is seen without a Trade Post nearby.

A. One line

Under the balance: "Lottery pot 48,210 · you are in".

B. A small card

A boxed card with the pot in amber, your chance and the last winner.

C. A LOTTERY key

The pot in one line, and a LOTTERY key in the key column that opens the same LOTTERY page as the Trade Post, without the traders band.

5. The draw in chat

The same in every look, on every server of the network, one line a second. The winner also gets a line of their own (and a Discord message if linked); a winner who is offline is told at their next login.
