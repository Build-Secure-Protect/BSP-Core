Crucible Yield Concepts

Mock-ups for the Crucible Yield Upgrade and the Tetrium Crucible's three upgrade slots. A crucible makes 4 Tetrium Nuggets per ore; each upgrade fitted adds 2, up to three, and the full set of three also doubles the slag. It always gives nuggets, never an ingot, so the Combination Forge still has its job.

1. The upgrade's icon

Each idea is shown large, then at its real size in slots beside the items it sits with: the Illyrium Forge Upgrade, RF Upgrade Mk I, a Tetrium Nugget and Raw Tetrium. Underneath is its tooltip. The icon is a 16 x 16 item texture, written by the asset generator.

A. A chip, like the forge upgrade

The Illyrium Forge Upgrade's grey chip with its red corner pins, but with a Tetrium Nugget in the middle and a turquoise arrow above it. The two upgrades read as one family.

B. A ladle pouring nuggets

A small slag-dark crucible with an orange glowing rim, tipping a thin stream of molten Tetrium onto two nuggets. It shows what the upgrade does: more metal out of the same pot.

C. A pile of nuggets on a tray

Three Tetrium Nuggets stacked on a slag tray, with a turquoise plus in the corner. Simple at a glance in a slot: more nuggets.

2. The three slots on the crucible's screen

The machine screen puts every upgrade in its UPGRADES row at the bottom right, where the crucible's RF slot already sits. The three yield slots join that row. Everything else is the screen as it is now: ore on the left, nuggets and slag on the right, the dial, the HEAT, TANK and RF gauges, PORTS, STATUS and the POWER switch. The crucible here runs on lava with an RF Upgrade fitted.

Correction to the first plan: the screen has no slots under the dial. Slots are placed by role, and upgrades always go in the UPGRADES row.

A. Four in a row, RF first

The RF slot stays where it is and the three yield slots follow it, all 20 pixels apart. Nothing else changes, so it is the smallest change. The RF slot and the yield slots look alike.

B. Yield slots together, a gap, then RF

The three yield slots touch, like an inventory row, with small YIELD and RF captions beneath. A gap separates them from the RF slot, because the two do different jobs.

C. As B, plus a yield readout

B's row, plus a line under it that says what one ore gives right now ("6 NUGGETS + 1 SLAG / ORE"). Three small pips under the nugget slot light up for each upgrade fitted, and turn orange when the full set doubles the slag.

What is the model and what is drawn

  • Icon: a flat 16 x 16 item texture, no 3D model.
  • Slots: three UPGRADE slots, one upgrade each, filled by hand only (pipes cannot insert or take them), in any order.
  • B and C need a small change to how the menu places upgrade and RF slots. C's readout and pips are drawn by the screen from the fitted upgrades.
