Vendor Post Looks

The Vendor Post is BSP-Core's own vendor: a one-block post an admin places and right-clicks to assign a shop and its opening times. It powers the block beneath with redstone while the shop is open (for signs and other cosmetics), and it draws the vendor figure on top. Easy NPCs are the other kind of vendor and bring their own looks; these three are for the post. The grey cube is one block for scale. Drag to turn; toggles show open and closed, and an unassigned post.

Decided (owner, 2026-10-11)

None of the three figures. The post draws a stock game model chosen per post: the villagers (every profession and biome type), the wandering trader and other vanilla mobs from a config list. No floating name (the owner uses signs). The head follows the nearest player. Unassigned: the post alone with its lamp blinking red, no figure until a shop is assigned. The post itself (lamp, name plate, redstone below) stays as drawn here.

1. Three figures

2. What all three share

  1. The post: a 16 x 16 block, the figure stands on it and reaches two blocks high in all; right-click the post or the figure to open the shop (or, as an admin, the assignment panel).
  2. An open lamp on the post lights while the shop is open and the post gives strong redstone power to the block below; closed: lamp off, no power.
  3. Unassigned: the figure holds a "?" board and the post's lamp blinks red; admins get the shop picker on right-click, players a "no shop here yet" line.
  4. The figure turns its head toward the nearest player within six blocks and idles otherwise; it never moves off the post and cannot be hurt.
  5. Built with a static post model from the generator and a renderer for the figure (boxes and planes, no entity, no AI), so it costs nothing to run and survives restarts and chunk reloads.

3. Decisions

  1. Figure: A Hooded Merchant, B Brass Automaton, or C Market Clerk.
  2. Name tag: the shop's name floats over the figure like a player's name (default), or only shows when looked at.
  3. Head turn: follows the nearest player (default), or still.
  4. Unassigned look: the "?" board and a blinking red lamp (default), or the figure missing (post only) until assigned.
