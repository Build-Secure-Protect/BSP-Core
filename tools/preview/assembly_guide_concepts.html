Assembly Guide Concepts

Three ways the in-game Assembly Guide could show how a multiblock goes together. All three are working mock-ups: pick a machine, press the buttons, drag a model to turn it. In game this would open from an unfinished controller and from the guide book.

Machine

A. Step Builder

One step at a time: the blocks for the current step pulse, earlier ones are solid, later ones are faint ghosts. A parts list on the right counts what you have placed so far. The closest to a checklist; good for building along with it open.

ASSEMBLY GUIDE

    B. Exploded Layers

    The whole machine at once, pulled apart into its layers. Pick a layer to light it up and list exactly what goes on it. Fastest to read if you already know roughly what you are building; nothing animates unless you move the slider.

      C. Ponder Scene

      The Create approach: a short scene plays, blocks drop into place group by group, and a caption explains each one. A scrubber lets you jump around. The most fun to watch and the clearest for a first-time builder; least useful as a checklist.
