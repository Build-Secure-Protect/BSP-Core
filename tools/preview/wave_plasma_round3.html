Wave Plasma Round 3

Your picks applied: ion-blue plasma that moves like a liquid, the Vortex Drum extractor with windows, three new pipe-routing interfaces, a subtler pump. Then the next group: the Battery Charger, Plasma Batteries, Power Cells and the Wave Emitter, with their screen, numbers and recipes. Drag any 3D view to turn it.

1. The plasma itself

Ion blue, drawn as a moving liquid: a shifting pattern of deep blue, ion blue and a bright core, like water with light in it. In game this is an animated fluid texture (16 x 16, 32 frames) used in tanks, pipes, windows and screens. Left: a tank of it. Right: a cable carrying it.

2. Plasma Extractor: Vortex Drum, detailed

The drum rebuilt with more going on: a bolted top collar with four intake pipes up to the totem, ribbed drum bands, four round portholes showing the plasma swirling, a gauge, outlet flanges with valve handles on each face, and a bottom manifold that the next drum's intakes meet. Two stacked under a totem.

The swirl turns faster as the drum fills. The portholes are the fill gauge: the top one only lights when the drum is nearly full.

3. Plasma Interface: three pipe-routing designs

Each block is a nest of pipes stepping down in size: a large trunk takes the plasma from the extractor, branches split it, and small nozzles on the outer faces are where cables plug in. Joined blocks share their trunks. Shown: four blocks on one extractor.

4. Plasma Repeater: pump, subtler

The rings swell by a third as much as before and travel a little; the wave reads as pumping without jumping.

5. Battery Charger

A block fed by Plasma Cable from an interface. A Plasma Battery or Power Cell goes in; it fills with plasma and is stamped with the powers chosen on the charger's screen, from those the interface offers. Three designs.

6. Plasma Batteries (four tiers)

A battery is a plasma tank that also holds powers. Placed on an extractor instead of a totem, it feeds the interface like a totem would, until it runs dry. Three design families, each shown in its four tiers: Tetrium, Magnatite, Illyrium, Charged Illyrium.

7. Power Cells and the Wave Emitter

A Power Cell is a small battery for carrying: charged the same way, it holds one, two or three powers by tier. The Wave Emitter is the held device that runs on a cell and gives its carrier those powers, as if they carried the totem. Three designs for each; the emitter is shown with a cell fitted.

8. The Battery Charger screen

One screen, in the Holo style. Click the powers to stamp them; the count allowed depends on what is in the slot.

Left: the charger's own tank and the item filling. Right: the powers the interface offers, with a STAMP switch each, up to the item's limit. A stamped power is kept in the item until it is emptied and charged again.

A battery feeds at the base totem rate, so a Tier IV keeps one projector running for most of an hour after a totem is stolen. Several batteries on several extractors add up. A battery keeps its stamped powers at the level the totem had when it was charged. Charging takes as long as the plasma takes to flow in: a Tier IV from one Tier I totem is 8 hours, so the Output upgrade matters here.

The emitter draws 20 mB/t while switched on (right-click to switch; it is off in the inventory, on in the offhand only). It gives its carrier the stamped powers at their stamped level, as the totem's Carried powers work today. A cell can only hold Carried powers (Damage, Resistance, Mining Speed, Swiftness, Vitality, Featherfall, Night Sight, and the new ones); Base powers stay with batteries.

Charged Resonance Crystal

Every battery and cell needs Resonance Crystals magnetised in the Magnetic Centrifuge: a new job, one Resonance Crystal and one Magnatite Nugget in, one Charged Resonance Crystal out, 30 seconds a try, quicker with more layers like the other jobs.
