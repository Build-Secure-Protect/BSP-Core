Vault Prize Concepts

Three concepts each for how the Coin Vault shows the new interest and the 12-hour prize rolls, and for the admin screen where the prize table is edited. They follow your answers: the rate rises with the hoard, a weighted table, one item a roll, 32 coin value to qualify, up to 6 rolls waiting, rolled when you press Redeem.

The example player holds 24 Diamond and 4 Netherite coins (128 coin value, so the ×2 step). They have 25 XP levels, 76 Tetrium and 2 Illyrium Ingots waiting (one day's worth), 2 prize rolls waiting and the next one due in 5:12:40. Item pictures here are stand-ins; the game draws the real ones.

1. The Coin Vault

Today the Storage tab has a two-line strip under the coins: XP, then Tetrium and Illyrium, with a bar for how full the 3-day cap is and the Redeem key. Each concept adds the multiplier and the prize rolls.

A. A taller strip

The strip gets a third line for prize rolls, in turquoise, and the multiplier joins the first line. Six pips under Redeem count the waiting rolls. The screen grows 10 pixels taller so nothing touches the inventory label.

B. An Interest tab

A fourth tab, INTEREST, explains everything: a ladder of the four steps with your hoard marked on it and how much more reaches the next step, what you earn a day, what is waiting, and the prize rolls with their countdown. The Storage strip stays as it is today with one word added for prizes.

C. A badge and a tooltip

The strip keeps its size. A gold ×2 badge sits beside the page line, and a prize ticket with its count sits left of Redeem. Hovering over the strip shows the full breakdown in a tooltip.

After Redeem (all three)

The interest line in chat stays as it is today. Each prize roll adds a line of its own in turquoise, and a rare prize (weight 5 or less) also plays the level-up sound. Items go to the inventory, or drop at your feet if it is full.

2. The prize table in the admin panel

An admin puts real items in, so enchanted books or named gear work too. Moderators see the table but cannot change it. The game shows every weight as a chance: weight divided by the total of all weights.

A. Prize shelf

A key on the SEASON tab opens a shelf like Season Rewards: 24 slots in three rows, with the weight printed under each slot. You drag items in from your inventory. Click a weight to pick it, then scroll or use the − and + keys. The line at the foot says what the picked prize is and its chance.

B. Prize list

A new VAULT tab works like KITS: the list of prizes on the left with each one's chance, the chosen prize on the right with its count and weight. FROM MY HAND copies the item you are holding, enchantments and all; PICK FROM LIST opens the kit editor's item browser.

C. Odds board

A new VAULT tab shows the whole table as one bar split by chance, then a tile per prize with − and + on its weight. TEST 100 ROLLS rolls the table a hundred times without giving anything, so you can check the odds feel right before saving.
