Stall Block Looks

One stall block, several skins, so it can match the shop it stands in. A stall is tied to one shop; right-click opens it. It carries a stock lamp (green while the shop has stock, red when its limited items are all out), the open lamp and the redstone signal below like the post, and a broken, out-of-order variant of its skin while no shop is assigned. Three shapes to choose from; pick a skin to see each shape wear it. The grey cube is one block for scale.

Skin:

1. Three shapes

2. Skins and states

  1. Fantasy: dark oak frame, violet and gold cloth, a banner; Sci-fi: steel and white panels, a cyan light strip, a holo pane; Steampunk: copper and brass, rivets, a pressure dial; Vanilla: oak planks, cobble feet, a plain sign. The same block in four palettes and trims; later skins are one more palette each.
  2. Broken: the awning torn or the pane cracked, boards loose, lamps dark, an OUT OF ORDER tag; this is what a stall shows while no shop is assigned (admins right-click to assign).
  3. Stock lamp: green while any item in the shop has stock (unlimited shops always), red when every limited item is out; the Jade line says which.
  4. Open lamp and redstone: like the Vendor Post: lit and powering the block below while the shop is open; off and no power when closed.

3. Decisions

  1. Shape: A Counter, B Kiosk, or C Cart.
  2. Skin set: the four named (default), or add a fifth now (name it).
  3. Stock lamp: a lamp on the stall (default), or the display item itself fading when out of stock.
  4. Display item: the stall shows its shop's first item floating above the counter (default), or no item.
