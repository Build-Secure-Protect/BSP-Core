Coin Re-press Concepts

Three ways the Shatter Coin Factory screen could take coins to re-press. A coin pressed outside its owner's team now goes back through a slice. It takes half the press time and half the RF of a new coin of its tier, and comes out stamped for the factory owner's current team.

The example: Mrgregles is in Iron Wolves (a team of 4) and owns a machine of 6 slices. Slices 1 to 3 press Gold blanks. Slice 4 re-presses 3 Gold coins pressed before Mrgregles joined (one is on the press, 2 wait). Slice 5 needs a blank. Slice 6 re-presses a Copper coin. Each mock-up shows the tooltip for slice 4. Item pictures are stand-ins; the game draws the real ones.

A. Same slot, violet lane

A coin goes into the blank slot, like a blank. The lane shows it is re-pressing: a violet RE under the slice number, and a violet bar. The empty slot's ghost keeps showing a blank. Nothing else on the screen changes.

  • Quickest to use: shift-click puts coins that need re-pressing into free lanes, as it does with blanks today.
  • A slice holds blanks or coins, not both at once.

B. A switch on each lane

Each lane has a small key between its slot and its bar: PR (press blanks) or RE (re-press coins). In RE the slot's edge turns violet, its ghost shows a coin, and it takes coins only. The bar is a little shorter to make room for the key.

  • Nobody drops a coin in by mistake: a lane takes one kind only.
  • One click per lane to switch, and the slot must be empty to switch.

C. A RE-PRESS tab with its own queue

Two tabs beside the title: PRESS (today's screen) and RE-PRESS. On RE-PRESS each lane has a second, violet slot for coins, so a slice holds blanks and coins at once. It re-presses its coins first (they are quicker), then goes back to its blanks. A small number under each lane on the other tab says what is waiting there.

  • Re-pressing never means taking blanks out first.
  • Each slice keeps a third slot; the screen gets tabs.

For all three: a slot takes only coins that need re-pressing (pressed for another team, or outside a team, while the owner is in one). Coins already pressed for the team, and coins with no stamp, are refused, as they work on the team's totems already. The tray takes the re-pressed coin as it takes a new one.
