Decoy Totem Concepts

A fake totem you craft and place to mislead Totem Compasses. It needs power from below, can be trapped, and breaks when an enemy tries to steal it. Three looks for the block and three layouts for its screen. Drag the 3D views to turn them. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

1. The block

2. The screen

What the owner sees on right-click. The main difference between the three is how upgrades are added.

A. Status Card

One small panel: power, compass range, trap and a Repair button. Upgrades are bought here with Shatter Coins, one button each. Simple, but there is little room to grow.

B. Trap Bench

Upgrades are crafted items put into three slots, like RF Upgrades in a machine: a range coil, a trap charge and a casing. Take them out to move them to another decoy. Fits the "hard to craft" idea best, and gives the new Magnatite parts more uses.

C. Mini Tree

A small version of the totem's upgrade tree: three short paths (Range, Trap, Toughness) bought with Shatter Coins and XP. Familiar, and it makes a decoy an investment, but an upgraded decoy cannot hand its upgrades to another.

How a decoy would work

Fooling compasses

  1. A Totem Compass tracking rivals that comes within the decoy's range points at the decoy instead of a real totem.
  2. The owner's own compass is never fooled by their own decoys.
  3. Once the decoy is broken or loses power, compasses go back to real totems.

Power and limits

  1. Needs RF fed in from the block below; with none it goes dark and fools nobody.
  2. 5 decoys per player.
  3. Cannot be stolen. It gives no score and no buffs.

Steal attempts and traps

  1. An enemy who tries to steal it finds out it is fake: the decoy breaks.
  2. A trapped decoy also explodes or curses the thief (a debuff) as it breaks.
  3. A broken decoy does nothing until its owner repairs it with materials.
