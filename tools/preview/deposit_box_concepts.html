Safety Deposit Box Looks

Three designs for the Safety Deposit Box block: one block, placed by admins, right-click opens the rent screen and then your box. Every player has one box for the whole network, so any of these blocks opens yours; the block shows your state when you look at it. All three sit in the steel-and-amber family of the chosen Vault Pillar ATM. The plain grey cube is one block, for scale. Drag to turn.

Decided (owner, 2026-10-11)

C, Drawer Stack, with gold trim: gold edges round each drawer front, gold handle ends and label plates, a gold line along the top and bottom of the cabinet. Chosen because it does not feel like the Coin Vault. Shows your own box state; red on the block plus a chat line at login when the rent has run out; latch click on opening, clunk on paying. A and B are not chosen.

3. Decisions

  1. Look: A Lockbox Wall, B Strongbox, or C Drawer Stack.
  2. Per-viewer state: the block shows your own box's state (default), or the same neutral look to everyone.
  3. Expired warning: red lamp or tag on the block (default) and a chat line at login saying the rent ran out; or the block only.
  4. Sound: a latch click on opening and a heavier clunk on paying (default), or silent.
