Bank UI Concepts

Three looks for the banking screens, chosen to sit apart from the machines' Holo HUD (dark navy, turquoise, gold, pixel type). Each panel is the same screen drawn in a skin: the ATM's account screen, the pay screen, the transaction log and the safety deposit box. In game the layout is drawn with textures and the Minecraft font; the mock-ups show material, palette, where things sit and how states read, not the exact lettering. The screens are one layout with swappable skins, so a stall can pick its own look later.

Decided (owner, 2026-10-11)

  • Skin B, Cash Machine. Passbook and Market Board stay on this page as candidate vendor skins.
  • Layout (revised twice with the owner): all keys in one column on the right, navigation at the top (Pay players, Transaction log) and the screen's own actions at the bottom (Deposit, Withdraw); the screen fills the left and middle; the status strip (bank, server, sync) runs across the whole bottom.
  • Coins: all five tiers with counts; green outline with the tier's own colour in the centre; a broken outline and no fill when the bank holds none of that tier.
  • Portable item: Portable Banker. Same screens, a Shopping Network side key instead of the deposit keys.
