Score Board Concepts

BSP-Teleport's request R8: other mods register boards and the Score Screen shows them, alone or in a rotation with the leaderboard. The sample board is BSP-Teleport's "Home servers": one line per restricted server, how many players call it home, how many are full, how many renting, with the server's Access Token as the icon. Three looks for the board page, each with its rotation indicator; the fourth card is the settings that drive it. Drawn as a 6 x 3 screen in the renderer's palette.

Rows like the ranked list: icon, the line's text, its value in gold at the right and the rest muted. Scrolls when there are more lines than rows, at the screen's scroll speed. The dots under the title are the rotation: three pages, this one lit. The most like the leaderboard, so a screen that turns between them keeps one voice.

No page swap: the leaderboard keeps two thirds of the screen and the right column turns through the registered boards, compact, with a "1/2" counter. Both always visible; needs a screen at least four blocks wide, and the board's extra text ("3 full · 2 renting") is dropped for the value alone.

Two rows join the existing settings: "Board" picks one registered board for a still screen, and "Rotate" turns the "In rotation" chips and the seconds on. Rules and Prizes start outside the rotation; every registered board starts inside it. Layout, font, size and speed still apply to the scoreboard page (and to a board's scrolling).

3. Picks for this round

  1. Board page look: A Ledger (default), B Tiles, or C Side column.
  2. Rotation indicator: dots under the title (A, default) or a draining bar (B). C has its counter.
  3. In the rotation by default: Scoreboard and every registered board; Rules and Prizes off (default). Alternative: everything on.
  4. Seconds per page: 10 by default, 5 to 120, with a 0.5 s cross-fade.
  5. Title strip: the board's own title on its page, the screen's title on the scoreboard page (default). Alternative: the screen's title always.
  6. Missing board (the mod is not on this client): "Board not installed here" in muted text and the rotation skips it next time round (default). Alternative: skip silently.
