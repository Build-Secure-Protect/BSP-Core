Shop Screen Concepts

What a player sees at a vendor, a stall or on the Shopping Network, drawn on the three built-in style presets. The presets are the bank's three skins: Cash Machine (the base you chose for the bank and the configurator), Passbook and Market Board. An admin assigns a preset per shop and can save palette and layout variations as presets of their own. Views: the shop page, the quantity screen for a buy or a sell, the sell GUI you drop items into, and a rotating or special-offer page. The admin key appears for admins and moderators.

Decided (owner, 2026-10-11)

Cards show the icon, "buy" beside the buy price, the sell price and the stock badge; a shop can be buy-only. The drop-in grid is called Sell here. Keys as shown. A closed shop shows no contents: only its name, CLOSED, when it opens and the time left. Cash Machine is built first; the other presets follow.

Screen: State:

1. The three presets

2. Rules every preset carries

  1. An item card shows the icon, the buy price (gold) and the sell price (dim) in its currency; a count badge for stock left (global, or yours); greyed when out of stock; a chip for instant buy.
  2. Left click a card: the buy quantity screen; right click: the sell quantity screen; shift-right click: sell all of that item from your inventory (the chosen ESGUI mappings, configurable per shop later).
  3. Quantity screen: -1, +1, +16, +32, Max (what you can afford and what fits, or what you hold for a sell), the total, Confirm, Cancel. Pay only for what fits.
  4. Sell here: drop items in; closing sells everything this shop buys at its sell prices and hands back the rest; the total updates as you drop.
  5. Sub-sections appear as cards with a folder mark; the header shows the path; Back climbs one level. Pages have Previous and Next.
  6. Rotating shops show "next rotation in" and only the drawn items; special offers show the chip with the percent and the end time, prices already reduced.
  7. A closed shop shows no contents: its name, CLOSED, when it opens and the time left; the Shopping Network list greys closed shops. A buy-only shop shows no sell prices and no Sell here or Sell all keys.
  8. Admins and moderators see an ADMIN key: it opens the configurator on this shop (moderators read-only) and the vendor's assignment and times.

3. Decisions

  1. Card content: icon, buy price, sell price, stock badge (shown), or icon and buy price only with the rest in the tooltip.
  2. Sell GUI: a 14-slot drop-in grid as shown, or the whole player inventory with a Sell all key.
  3. Keys on the shop page: Back, page keys at the top; Sell GUI, Sell all, Admin at the bottom (shown), or page keys below the grid.
  4. Build order: Cash Machine first, the other two presets once the screens work (default), or all three together.
