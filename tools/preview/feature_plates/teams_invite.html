Team Popup Concepts

Mock-up set 2 of the Teams line: the invite popup an invited player sees, and the badge that shows while their chat goes to the team. Both are drawn on a mock game view with the hotbar, the chat and the existing BSP overlays in their real places (the Recall offer sits 100 px above the bottom, centred; the Sentinel strip slides in at the top left). Section 3 shows the Team screen look C refined to the picks from set 1: member rows and the board as its own MESSAGES view.

1. The invite popup

The invite arrives from Avenn for team Nightwatch. It lasts 60 s; the player answers with two keys (shown as R and N, the Recall offer's keys, rebindable in Controls) or clicks the chat line. The three placements differ in where the panel sits and how it counts down. In every one, a second prompt (here a Recall offer) queues behind or beside it instead of covering it.

2. The team chat badge

While TEAM CHAT is on, everything the player types goes to the team. The badge is the reminder. It is drawn in the team's colour (aqua here) and disappears when the player switches back. /t message sends one line without switching; a team line reads [Team] Avenn: message in the team colour.

B1. Pill above the chat

A small pill sitting just above the chat lines, visible whether the chat box is open or not. Clear at a glance before typing.

B2. Tag in the chat box

Nothing on the HUD; the chat box itself carries a team-coloured edge and "to Nightwatch" in front of the text when it is open. Zero clutter in play, seen exactly when it matters: as you type.

B3. Tag by the hotbar

<Pell> anyone selling lava?

TEAM

A tag to the right of the hotbar, where the eye goes for the item in hand. Always visible while on, away from the chat column; the team name shows when the chat box opens.

4. Decisions for this round

  1. Popup placement: P1 the Recall's spot (stacking upward), P2 the top-left strip, or P3 the right-edge card. Default: P1, one prompt panel for Recall and invites alike.
  2. Keys: share the Recall offer's accept and decline keys (R and N today, rebindable) for every prompt, so a player learns two keys. Default: yes. Alternative: a separate pair for team prompts.
  3. Chat line as well: every invite also prints the clickable line in chat, which is how a player in an inventory or another screen answers (HUD panels do not draw over screens). Default: yes.
  4. Sound: a quiet note when an invite arrives (a note block chime at low volume). Default: yes.
  5. Badge: B1 the pill above the chat, B2 the tag in the chat box, or B3 the tag by the hotbar. Default: B1.
  6. What the inviter sees: one chat line when the invite is accepted, declined or runs out, and the "waiting" row on the invite view meanwhile. Default: yes.
  7. Refined C: the members view with totem counts and the hint line, the MESSAGES view with the badge on the key, as drawn. Default: yes.
