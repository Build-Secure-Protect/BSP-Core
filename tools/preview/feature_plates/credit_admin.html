Store Credit Concepts

Three concepts each for the ticket, the admin panel's CREDIT tab, what a player sees when paying with credit, and the notice when a ticket's clock starts or runs out. They follow your round 1 answers: kinds made in the admin screen, a colour and a C or D mark per kind, a scrolling list of plot names, credit paying first with the bank paying the rest, a notice the player can't ignore, and a ticket that disappears when it runs out.

The examples: Donor 10,000 (purple, D, 30 days, bound when given, spends at Spawn Market, any Land Office plot and teleports), Sanctuary Plot (lime, C, 6,000, 14 days, Sanctuary plots only), Market Gift (orange, C, 2,000, no time limit) and Teleport Pass (light blue, D, 1,500, 7 days, teleports only). Pick a colour and a mark below to see every ticket shape in it.

1. The ticket

Each shape is one 16 by 16 item texture, built from two layers. A grey body layer is tinted in the kind's colour, so all 16 dye colours come from one texture. The gold, gem and print details stay untinted. The C or D mark sits in a third layer picked by the item's model, top left as you asked. Below each big icon are all 16 colours with a C and with a D, and a hotbar at real game size.

A. Admit-one stub

A classic paper ticket with a notch in each end, a perforated tear line and a gold star on the stub. The mark is a black tab over the top left corner, with a white C or a gold D. It reads as "ticket" even at hotbar size.

B. Gift card

A rounded plastic card with a gold chip, a Shatter Piece gem and an embossed number line. The mark is a round gold seal with a dark letter. It looks like money, and the gem ties it to the bank's pieces.

C. Sealed voucher

A tall slip of paper torn at both ends, with three printed lines and a gold wax seal. The mark is a white sticker with a blue C or a red D, so the two kinds differ in colour as well as letter.

The tooltip (all three)

Your answer 20, the same for every shape. On the left is a ticket before its first purchase; on the right is one partly spent and bound.

2. The CREDIT tab in the admin panel

A fifth tab after KITS. To fit five tabs the tabs narrow from 46 to 40 pixels. The list of kinds is on the left, as the players list is on PLAYERS. The concepts differ in how the kind's settings and its places are laid out. The plot names list shows each name once with how many plots share it (your "groups"), scrolls, and has a box to type a name that isn't on this server.

A. A form, with the places in a pop-over

The form fits the panel's normal 344 by 210 size. "Spends at" shows a summary chip per place. EDIT opens a list over the form, with SHOPS, LAND OFFICE and TELEPORTS tabs, a search box and tick boxes, and DONE closes it.

B. A printed ticket on top, with every place in view

The top of the form is the ticket itself, printed large in its colour with its mark, name, value and time. It changes as you edit. Under it, the three place lists sit side by side and each scrolls on its own, so nothing is hidden behind a key. The panel grows to 290 pixels tall while a kind is open, as the kit editor does.

C. Three steps: look, value and time, places

The form is split into three steps with NEXT and BACK keys. Step 2 ends with a sentence that says what the ticket will do. Step 3 is one long list with Shops, Land Office and Teleports as headings. It keeps the normal panel size and each step has plenty of room.

The ticket list (all three)

TICKETS opens every ticket of the kind that has been given, from your answer 23. Each row shows the serial, the holder (or "unbound"), the credit left and the time left. VOID ends a ticket, and its item disappears the next time it is seen.

3. Paying with credit

A shop's quantity screen and the Land Office's plot panel, while a ticket that works there is in the player's inventory. In the shop, a Donor 10,000 ticket with 2,000 left meets a 2,500 purchase, so the bank pays the other 500 (answer 9). At the Land Office, a Sanctuary Plot ticket pays the whole 6,000 for Sanctuary 4. The switch is on by default (answer 10).

A. A lit CREDIT key on the status strip

A green key at the right end of the status strip, the shop's own ON key style. Click it to switch credit off (cream) or back on. The total gains a second line with the split, and the strip shows the credit left beside the balance.

B. The ticket on the screen, with a receipt

The ticket appears on the green screen with a tick box under it, and the total becomes a short receipt: price, credit, bank. With two tickets the screen shows the one that pays first, plus "and 1 more".

C. The status strip becomes the ticket

While credit is on, the whole status strip turns into the ticket, with notched ends, its colour and mark, the credit left and the time left. Click the strip to switch credit off, and it goes back to the plain green strip. The total reads as one sum, "2,500 = 2,000 credit + 500 bank".

Teleports (all three)

BSP-Teleport has no BSP-Core screen of its own, so a teleport ticket simply pays first (question 27). The chat line says so. These lines are BSP-Teleport's words once it takes request SC-1.

4. The clock starts, and the ticket runs out

On the first purchase with a timed ticket, the player is told in a way they can't miss (answer 8). When the time runs out, they are told and the ticket disappears from their inventory (answer 3). Each concept shows both. The game view behind is a stand-in.

A. A full-screen stamp

The screen washes in the ticket's colour, the ticket drops in large with a stamp sound, and the words stay for 6 seconds before fading. The player can still move. When the ticket runs out, it crumbles away into pixels.

B. A screen they must close

A small window opens over the game. Its I UNDERSTAND key is grey for 3 seconds with a countdown, and Esc does nothing until then. It can't be missed, but it can stop a player in the middle of a fight, so it waits until they have been out of combat for 5 seconds.

C. A big title and a bar that stays

A big title and subtitle in the middle, the same as a vanilla title, then a bar at the top in the ticket's colour that counts down the time left. It stays for the first 10 minutes, and comes back for the last 24 hours and at every login. When it runs out, the bar drains, turns red and goes.

  • All three: a chat line as well ("Your Donor 10,000 ticket's 30 days have started: 8,000 pieces to spend by 7 December."), so the date is there to scroll back to. A player who is offline when a ticket runs out sees the notice at their next login.
