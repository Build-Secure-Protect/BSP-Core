Land Office Concepts

Three layouts for the plot shop screen BSP-Core adds for Region Guard (request RG-C3). All three sit in the Cash Machine skin, since every bank and shop screen does, and all three buy through Region Guard's own claim service, which keeps the rules and the claim. The screen opens from an admin-placed Land Office block, or with /bsp plots for an Easy NPC, the same way shops do.

What is shown in every mock-up

Example plots: Harbour Row 3 (20 x 20, 4,000 pieces, for sale), Hilltop 7 (30 x 30, 9,000 pieces, for sale), Common Field (an open area where a player marks their own ground: 12 pieces a block, 10 to 40 a side), and the player's own Harbour Row 1. The balance in the strip is the bank's.

Two panes: the list (plots for sale in amber, open areas with their price per block, your own claims dim at the bottom) and the chosen one's details with the quote: size, price, the rules that matter to a buyer, and your balance after. SHOW PLOT pulses the plot's gold outline in the world for ten seconds. For an open area the details pane reads your wand selection (Region Guard's /claim tool) as a size and a total. GIVE UP works on your own claims, shows the refund, and asks twice.

A top-down map of the plots around the player, drawn from the same listings the client already gets for the gold outlines: for sale in amber, yours in green, others' plain, open areas dashed, you as an arrow. Click a plot to select it; one line under the map carries the quote. Buying an open area draws your wand selection on the map as you make it. Strongest for seeing where a plot is; weakest for reading its rules, which need a second line or a tooltip.

3. Decisions, with defaults

  1. Layout. A Ledger (default), B Map board, or C Deed counter. A mix is possible: A with B's map in place of the details pane.
  2. Opening. Default: the Land Office block plus the command. Alternative: also a key on the Portable Banker, like the Shopping Network.
  3. Open ground. Default: the player marks it with Region Guard's wand and the screen prices the selection. Alternative: pick a width and depth in the screen, centred on where you stand.
  4. Give up. Default: in this screen, refund shown, two confirms. Alternative: leave it to Region Guard's command only.
  5. Your claims. Default: listed in the same screen (dim rows in A, green in B, a line in C) so ENLARGE and GIVE UP have something to point at.
