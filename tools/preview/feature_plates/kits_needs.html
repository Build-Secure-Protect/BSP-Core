Kit Conditions Concepts

Three mock-up sets for kit conditions: how an admin ticks conditions in the kit editor (the first one is "owns no totem"), how the kit's card looks to a player who still owns a totem, and the screen a player sees when a steal-back is refused. They follow your answers: a list of conditions, a new Tier I totem in the player's name, REDEEM only, and the card greyed with "You own a totem". The screens are drawn at GUI pixel size with the game's own font.

The kit in every picture is Second Chance: one Shatter Totem and 16 bread, once only, for players with no totem. Conditions marked example do not exist; they only show how each layout grows when more are added later.

1. Conditions in the kit editor

The editor's settings block is full today: Name, Describe, Given, Grant on, Browser and Take, Price and Servers, Ranks, Enabled. Every concept adds a Needs row under Ranks, so the panel grows by one row (310 to 325 pixels tall). A kit with no conditions shows "nothing", and anyone may take it.

A. A row of chips, like Ranks

Each condition is a short chip: click one to turn it on, and every chip that is on must be true. Turning on "nothing" clears them all. It looks just like the Ranks and Servers rows. More conditions mean more chips on the same row, and it wraps onto a second row once it is full.

B. A tick list in plain words

Each condition is a whole sentence with a tick box in front: "Owns no Shatter Totem anywhere on the network". The wording says exactly what the rule checks, and a condition that needs a number (days, totems) can have its own box on its line. The panel grows by one line for every condition added later.

C. A summary chip that opens a list

The Needs row shows a summary chip, "no totem ›". Clicking it opens a list over the item browser, with a tick box and a full sentence for each condition, and DONE closes it. The settings block grows by just the one row, however many conditions are added later.

2. The card while a player owns a totem

The kit wall with a ready Starter card next to Second Chance, locked because the player owns one placed totem. Next to them is the card turned over (click a card's icon in the game): its To get it list gains a line for each condition.

A. Greyed, like a redeemed kit

The card is grey, with the icon dimmed, a grey "You own a totem" chip and "for players with no totem" underneath. It reads like the Redeemed and cooldown cards.

B. Greyed, and it says where the totem is

The same grey card with an amber chip, plus a line naming where the player's totems are: "1 placed · survival". A player who forgot one in a chest or on another server sees why the kit is locked.

C. Greyed, with a crossed-out totem

The grey card with a red chip and a small totem struck through with a red line. The picture says "no totem" before anyone reads the words.

3. "naughty naughty"

A player who redeemed a totem kit tries to steal back a totem they used to own within 3 hours. Nothing starts and nothing names the reason: the words fill their screen for 5 seconds and then fade. The game view behind is a stand-in.

Loops every 6 seconds: 5 on screen, 1 off.

A. A red stamp

The whole screen turns dark red and "NAUGHTY NAUGHTY" lands in huge white letters with a thump and a short shake. It fades out over the last second.

B. A wagging finger

The screen dims and a big pixel hand wags its finger from side to side over "naughty naughty" in gold. It's cheeky rather than angry.

C. Warning tape

Rows of red "NAUGHTY NAUGHTY" tape scroll across the whole screen in both directions, with a black band through the middle carrying the words in large white letters.

  • All three: drawn over the game and the HUD for 5 seconds. The player can still move and fight (question 16), and another refused attempt while it is showing does not start it again.
