Herald Tree Concepts

Mock-up set 2: where Herald sits on the upgrade tree. Each tree is drawn from the game's own layout numbers (the five tier rings, the six path spokes, 14-pixel nodes), on a tier III totem that has Alarm at level 2, with Herald selected and its card on the right, using the link line you picked under Buy. Herald is a Base power, so its node is gold like the other Walls upgrades. Its letters are HR, because Healing already uses HE.

A slip in my earlier question: it said "tier II, beside Sentinel", but Sentinel sits on the tier III ring. Option A keeps tier II; options B and C put Herald beside Sentinel on tier III.

A. Tier II, on Alarm's own ring

Herald sits on the tier II ring just above Alarm, a little off the Walls spoke, the way Bouncy sits beside Featherfall. Nothing else moves.

Opens
at tier II, once Alarm reaches level 2
Price
2 Gold Shatter Coins and 6 XP levels (Sentinel's Raid prices)
Closest node
Alarm, 18 pixels apart

B. Tier III, the other side of Ward

Herald sits on the tier III ring on the far side of Ward, mirroring Sentinel. Ward and Sentinel stay where players know them.

Opens
at tier III, once Alarm reaches level 2
Price
2 Diamond Shatter Coins and 9 XP levels
Closest node
Deadlock, 17 pixels apart

C. Tier III, straight out from Alarm

Herald takes the spot straight out from Alarm on the Walls spoke, and Ward moves across to the other side. This places Herald most clearly, but players will find Ward in a new place.

Opens
at tier III, once Alarm reaches level 2
Price
2 Diamond Shatter Coins and 9 XP levels
Closest node
Alarm, 15 pixels apart
