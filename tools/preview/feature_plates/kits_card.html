Kits: the refined screens

The owner's picks drawn together. Admin side: the KITS tab lists the kits beside a detail pane (look B), and EDIT or NEW opens the workbench editor (look A) with mod chips over the item browser and RankUp's ladder as the rank picker. Player side: the card wall (look B), the reason on each card, a redeemed kit greyed with the time left, REDEEM on a ready card, a waiting kit in violet with TAKE IT, and the header line. Drawn at one and a half times the in-game size; icons are stand-ins.

1. The KITS tab on the admin panel

The panel stays 344 x 210. Kits on the left, the chosen kit's summary on the right: its items, how it is given, the take rule, ranks, servers, price, and how often it was taken. GIVE TO… lists the players online here; DELETE asks SURE?. /bsp admin kits opens the panel on this tab.

2. The editor (workbench)

The panel grows to 344 x 290 while the editor is open. The kit's 36 slots top left (click takes a slot out, the wheel sets its count, shift-click clears it; FROM HAND and FROM INVENTORY bring in enchanted or named items). The item browser top right: a search line, the mod chips, a wheel-scrolled grid (click adds one, shift-click a stack, hover names the item). The settings rows under both; the Ranks row is RankUp's ladder drawn as steps, the chosen step and everything above it lit; without RankUp the row shows the LuckPerms group names as chips. SAVE writes the kit to every server; TEST ON ME gives the admin the kit at once with no record; DELETE asks SURE?.

3. The player's kit browser (card wall)

Opened by the KITS button above the inventory, a key binding or /kits. Four cards a row, the wheel scrolls when there are more than eight. Each card carries its reason: a ready kit has REDEEM on the card; a redeemed kit sits grey with the time left and brightens back when ready; a locked kit shows the rank step, the server, or the price with what you have; a waiting kit (a reward or grant that did not fit yet) is violet with TAKE IT and the slots it needs. Clicking a card's icon or name flips it to its items (below).
