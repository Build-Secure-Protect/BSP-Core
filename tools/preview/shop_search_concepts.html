Shop Screen Search

Spawn shops task 8 (SS-9). A search on the player's shop screen that looks through the shop and all its sub-sections at once, by the item's name as the player's game shows it, or by mod with "@create". Three places for the search box, on the Decor shop (five sections, 271 cards) with "oak" typed. Results fill the card grid; hovering one says which section it is in, and clicking it opens that section with the card picked out.

A. A Search key

A Search key in its own group between the page keys and the sell keys. Pressed, the header turns into the search line ("> DECOR search: oak_") with the count of results on the right; Back clears the search. The page header is unchanged until then. (With an admin's two extra keys the column is full; the key moves into the Admin group for them.)

B. A box in the header

A small boxed field always sits at the right of the header, in place of "page 1 / 13" while it holds text. Click it and type; the page count comes back when it is emptied. Nothing else on the screen moves.

C. The bottom strip

The strip under the screen ("open / shopping network") becomes the search line when clicked or when the player starts typing: "search: oak_ (9 found)", the balance staying on the right. The header keeps the shop's path.
