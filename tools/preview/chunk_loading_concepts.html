Chunk Loading Concepts

Working mock-ups for the totem's chunk screen and the new Totem Projector screen. Use the two controls to step through the upgrade levels, and click chunks in any mock to load or unload them. All six mocks share the same selection.

1. Totem chunk screen

Opened from a CHUNKS tab that appears in the totem panel's header once the Chunk Loader upgrade has a level. The gold marker is the totem; its chunk is always loaded and uses one of the chunk slots. North is up.

A. Map Tiles

A small top-down map of the real terrain, one tile per chunk, so you can recognise your base. Loaded chunks get a turquoise frame; chunks outside your range are dark.

B. Blueprint and List

A plain schematic grid with a list beside it. Each grid square and each list row says what is in the chunk (machines, projectors), and the list names the chunk coordinates. Remove a chunk from the list or the grid.

C. Range Rings

Matches the Tier Rings upgrade tree: the totem in the middle, one square ring per range level. Rings you have not unlocked are dashed. Chunk slots sit along the top like sockets. The smallest of the three screens.

2. Totem Projector screen

Right-click a projector. It shows the powers arriving from the generator and, when Chunk Loading is one of them, a chunk picker centred on the projector (violet marker). The projector's chunk count comes from the level that arrives down the cable.

A. Side by Side

Powers on the left, chunks on the right, everything visible at once.

B. Two Tabs

POWERS and CHUNKS tabs, like the Coin Vault. Each tab has more room; the CHUNKS tab only appears when Chunk Loading arrives. Click the tabs.

C. Status Strip

One column: a status line and RF bar, the arriving powers as coloured chips, and the chunk map underneath.
