Plasma Tank Concepts

A tank built from three blocks: Tank Casing for the edges, Tank Glass to close the faces, and Tank Ports wherever cables should meet it. Any box from 3 x 3 x 3 up to 12 x 12 x 12, hollow inside. It holds Wave Plasma only, 5,000,000 mB per block of the shell, and no powers. Three looks below, the same 6 x 5 x 6 tank with four ports in each. Drag to turn.

1. Three looks

2. How it fills

One surface for the whole tank, as a liquid: it rises evenly across every block at once. A port above the surface pours a visible stream down to it, with a ripple where it lands; a port below the surface shows nothing, the level just climbs. Draining through a port lowers the level the same way. Press Fill or Drain and watch; the right-hand tank has its port at the side and the left one at the top.

3. Ports

Left to right: a port set to In and out (turquoise ring, the default: it takes plasma from a run that comes from an interface and gives plasma to a run that only leads to bases, chargers or other tanks), Input (arrow pointing into the tank), Output (arrow pointing out), and a port with no cable. The wrench steps a port through the three settings; Jade and the wrench readout name the setting. Each port gives at most 1,000 mB/t, like an interface face, so a tank that feeds a big base needs several.

4. Rules

RuleProposed
ShapeA hollow box, 3 to 12 blocks on each side, any mix of the three. Every edge must be Tank Casing. Faces are Tank Glass, Tank Casing or Tank Ports in any mix. The inside must be empty.
FormsWhen the last block completes a valid shell it forms by itself: no controller. The lowest corner keeps the contents. Right-clicking any block shows a line with the level; Jade shows "Wave Plasma: 1,250,000 / 7,280,000 mB".
Capacity5,000,000 mB for every block in the shell (the Tier IV battery's figure). A 12 x 12 x 12 shell is 728 blocks: 3,640,000,000 mB.
BreakingBreak one block and the tank goes dormant but remembers its plasma; put the block back and it is whole again. Break it apart and the plasma is gone. Rebuild smaller and it keeps what fits, bigger and it keeps what it had.
ShownThe plasma fills the glass as one surface, drawn by the same renderer as the Projector Base. Casing and ports hide what is behind them, so a glass wall is where you read the level.
RecipesCasing: Tetrium Plates and a Machine Chassis. Glass: Illyrium Tank Glass round a Tetrium Plate. Port: a casing, a cable and a Charged Resonance Crystal, so it costs something to add many.
