Tripwire Concepts

Three mock-up sets for Tripwire, the totem power that gives a redstone signal while an outsider is near: where its node sits on the upgrade tree, the key that picks its range, and how the placed totem shows that it is tripped. Everything follows the defaults you took. The screens are drawn at GUI pixel size with the game's own font. The totem is the real model and texture.

A slip in my question 2: it said "Tier II, on the ring with Ward and Sentinel", but Ward and Sentinel sit on the Tier III ring. Tripwire has to start at Tier II for one range per tier to reach 128 at Tier V, so it goes on the Tier II ring, with Alarm and Herald. The three tree concepts are the places it fits there without touching another node.

SIGNAL 15

1. The node on the tree

A Tier III totem with Alarm at level 2 and Tripwire at level 1 (16 blocks), Tripwire selected. Its letters are TR. It is a Base power at Raid prices, so the next range costs 2 Diamond coins and 9 XP levels.

A. Between Alarm and Sanctuary

Tripwire sits on the Tier II ring below Alarm, towards the Home path, in the gap above Sanctuary. Nothing else moves.

B. Above Alarm, Herald moves down

Tripwire takes Herald's place just above Alarm, and Herald moves to the gap above Sanctuary. Tripwire then sits closest to Alarm, but players will find Herald somewhere new.

C. Place A, and the node shows the signal

The same place as A. While the totem is giving its signal, the node's frame turns red, so the tree also tells you someone is near. Untick the box at the top to see it go back to gold.

2. The range key under Buy

The same card one level on: 16 and 32 blocks are bought, so the next range (64) needs Tier IV and the reason sits under Buy, as it does for Herald. The range line goes where Herald's Discord line goes, between Buy and the tier box. The range is set to 32.

The red reason is cut to one line ("Raise the t..."), as Herald's is in game today, because the range line needs the second line's place. The build can shorten that reason for Tripwire to "Tier III limit. Raise to Tier IV", which fits whole.

A. One NEXT key

"Range: 32 blocks" with a small NEXT key on the right. Each press steps to the next bought range, then OFF, then back to 16.

B. A row of five chips

Five chips, 16, 32, 64, 128 and OFF. Click a chip to pick its range. Ranges not bought yet are greyed and do nothing. No label, because the chips say it all.

C. A signal lamp and a RANGE key

A small lamp on the left shows whether the signal is on right now ("SIGNAL ON" in red, or "signal off"). A RANGE 32 key on the right steps through the ranges like A's NEXT.

3. The tripped look on the totem

The placed totem with four upgrade orbs, wired with a line of redstone dust to a lamp, as a base would use it. Drag to turn it. Untick the box at the top to see it at rest. In each one the statue's model is unchanged except where it says baked.

A. Lamp studs round the base

Eight round red lenses, two on each side of the base, each set in a small square bezel. They glow while the signal is on and go dull dark red at rest.

  • Baked: the eight bezels (four thin bars each, standing a quarter pixel proud).
  • Renderer: the lenses, lit or dull.

B. A red line in a groove

A groove runs all round the base at half height, with a red light strip in it. While the signal is on, a brighter spot chases round the strip once a second.

  • Baked: the base split into a lower and an upper slab with a narrower core between them, which makes the groove.
  • Renderer: the strip and the chasing spot.

C. The orb turns red and pings

The orb in the statue's hands takes a red glow, and once a second a red ring spreads out along the floor from the base and fades, like a radar ping.

  • Baked: nothing.
  • Renderer: the red shell round the orb and the floor ring.
