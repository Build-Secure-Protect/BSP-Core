Card Note Lines

Spawn shops task 5 (SS-10). A card can carry a note of up to three short lines, shown in its tooltip on the shop screen and never put on the item the player gets. Three places and looks for it, on the weekly Challenge shop with Steel Casing hovered. The note here is two lines: "The Challenge buys up to 4 each per week." and "Used machines welcome; empty them first."

A. Under the name, like an item's own lore

The note sits right under the item's name and mod, in the purple italic the game uses for an item's lore, before the shop's own lines (price, match, limits, refill). It reads as part of the item's description.

B. A note at the foot

The shop's lines come first; after a blank line, the note closes the tooltip in amber, the Cash Machine's own colour, so it reads as the shopkeeper speaking.

C. A marked block between item and shop

Between the item's lines and the shop's, each note line starts with a grey bar and sits in plain white, set apart like a quoted remark.
