Plasma Interface Concepts

Interfaces that touch already work as one group of up to twelve, but each block still looks like a separate machine. These three skins make a touching group read as one body, the way an ME Controller does: the faces between joined blocks vanish, the frame runs only round the outside of the whole group, and the lit parts carry on across the joins. Every view shows the same six-block group with a drum on its left, cables leaving on two sides, and a thirteenth block on the right that is refused (its lit parts turn red). Drag to turn.

PartProposed
Joined facesThe block already knows what each of its six sides touches. A side touching another interface draws nothing, so the two bodies read as one.
FrameThe frame strips sit only on edges that are on the outside of the whole group. That needs the diagonal neighbours too, so the block gets a model that reads its neighbours when it is drawn (the way AE2 does it) instead of more blockstates.
Lit partsBlue while the group works, red on a block the group refuses (a thirteenth, or one touching a drum another group holds). Same rule as today's seam, carried into the skin.
Nozzles and tubesA cable nozzle appears on any outer face a cable touches; the tube into a drum stays as it is. Faces with nothing on them stay plain.
Screen and JadeUnchanged: right-click any block of the group for the 3D view; Jade shows the name.
