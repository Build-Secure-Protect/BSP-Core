Accelerator Ring, Round 2

The Arc Coils look, reworked: the eight blocks still have to be placed as a 3 x 3 ring, but once the ring forms their cubes vanish and the controller draws the whole machine as one shape. Three takes on that shape below, each with its de-accelerator beside it. The buttons step the machine through its three states: formed but without RF, RF connected (the coils wake), and a link established with plasma flowing (the full show). Drag a view to turn it.

3. Decisions, with defaults

  1. Shape. C1, C2 or C3. Default C1, Octagon Synchrotron: the octagon fills the 3 x 3 best and the chase of lights reads as "accelerator".
  2. Idle. Default: nothing moves without RF; with RF the ring wakes but the black hole only turns slowly; the full animation needs a link delivering.
  3. Sound. Default: a low hum while RF is connected, the portal ambient on top while delivering.
  4. Light. Default: light level 7 from the intake while delivering, 3 with RF only.
