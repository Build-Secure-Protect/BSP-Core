Remote Control Transmitter

The block that turns read-only Holo Grid boxes into ones you can operate. Place it near the tagged machines. While you, or anyone on your team, stand inside its range and have the Remote Control unlock, clicking a box opens that machine's own screen. The range is 16 blocks, plus 16 for each range card in its three slots (a stack each), up to 3072 blocks. It uses power while someone is operating a machine through it. Three things to pick: the block, the range card, and the screen.

Click a card slot in a screen to add 16 cards. A full slot empties again.

1. The block

Each block has three labelled keys on the front, CARDS, POWER and TEAM, the screen's own words, which open the screen on that page. Every look shows its card slots from the outside, so you can see at a glance that it has cards.

A. Radio Mast

Baked model: a steel base with a turquoise seam and a recessed front panel. Three card slits with green cards standing in them, a copper FE port, a mast with three steel rings, and corner bolts. Renderer: a small dish at the top that turns, and a red tip light that blinks while someone is operating.

B. Dish Station

Baked model: a stepped base with a round turntable, a front card drawer with three bays, and a round copper FE socket. A glass tube on the side shows the charge in gold. Renderer: a large dish on an arm that sweeps from side to side, with a turquoise feed horn that pulses.

C. Signal Lantern

Baked model: a lantern frame with four steel posts, an open glass drum, a capped top with the keys under the eave, and three card bays in the base. Renderer: a glowing turquoise orb that floats in the drum, with two rings turning around it faster while someone is operating.

2. The range card

A flat 16 × 16 item that stacks to 64. 2 copper ingots, 2 redstone, 1 quartz and 1 ender pearl make 2. Click one to see it in the screens below.

A. Punch Card

Cream card stock with a copper edge, a notched corner and a pattern of punched holes.

B. Circuit Card

A green circuit board with gold contacts along the bottom, a gold trace and a turquoise chip.

C. Crystal Chip

A pale glass slab with a glowing turquoise core, shaded on one side.

3. The screen

Every screen has the card slots, the range, the power (100,000 FE stored, 20 FE a tick plus 1 per card while someone operates through it), the team who may use it, what is happening now, and your inventory for moving cards in.

A. Rack

Three tall card slots on the left fill like gauges as the stacks grow. In the middle, RANGE shows the number of blocks, a bar to the 3072 cap and how many more cards reach it. On the right, POWER has a battery, the charge and the draw. WHO CAN USE IT lists the owner, with a crown, and each team member, with an online lamp. One line says who is operating what right now.

B. Map Table

A map of the land with the range drawn as a square around the transmitter, shown in gold. Tagged machines inside it show turquoise, and those outside it grey. A ripple runs out from the transmitter while someone is operating. On the right: range in blocks and its width, the three card slots, a power bar with the draw, and the team as a row of heads.

C. Signal Tower

A round dial in a steel plate. Rings mark the range on a stretched scale, so 16 and 3072 both read clearly. The gold power ring runs round the edge. The cards go in three cartridge bays with lamps under the dial. The team sits in a row of sockets with their heads. A NOW panel says who is operating and how much power it draws.

What every concept shares

PartBehaviour
RangeA cube around the block: 16 blocks with no cards, plus 16 per card, up to 3072 (191 cards reach it). Tagged machines inside it are linked, and a player inside it can operate them.
WhoThe player who placed it and their team, as long as they hold the Remote Control unlock. Allies cannot. Each player still tags their own machines.
PowerIt stores 100,000 FE and takes it from any side. It uses 20 FE a tick plus 1 per card while someone is operating a machine through it, and nothing while idle. Without enough FE, the boxes go back to read-only.
LoadedThe transmitter's chunk and the machine's chunk must be loaded, for example by an Anchor. An unloaded transmitter counts as off.
Placing and breakingOnly where you may build, up to 4 per player per server. Anyone who may break blocks there can break it, raiders included. It drops with its cards inside it.
ResetsA reset leaves transmitters and their cards as they are. The Remote Control unlock is what resets.
