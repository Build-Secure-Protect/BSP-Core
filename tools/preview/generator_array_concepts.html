Generator Array Concepts

Up to nine Totem Generators in a three by three layer, with the Shatter Totem on the middle one. Each generator must be under the totem or touch a generator that is already connected. They join into one block, and a ring pulses up around the totem, growing with the array until it is as wide as the full three by three. Three ways the joined generators could look, all based on the Siphon Plinth you picked. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

They are added in a sensible order here: the middle one first, then the four beside it, then the corners.

Reach: cable and generator together

The cable sets the longest run it can carry. The generator sets how far it can push. The run that works is the smaller of the two, so reaching 80 blocks needs both the best cable and a fully upgraded generator.
