BSP-Core · Remote Control · Inventory Link

Inventory Link looks

Operating a machine through a Remote Control Transmitter opens its real screen. Without the transmitter's Inventory Link upgrade, the player can move items inside the machine and use its switches, but their own inventory is closed off. Each concept below shows that locked inventory over a Mekanism Enrichment Chamber, and the matching upgrade panel in the empty space beside the transmitter's inventory. Click the buttons under each to see the upgrade bought, a team member's view, and what happens when the screen closes with an item held.

A · Lock plate

A dark steel plate is screwed over the inventory, with Holo Grid turquoise corners, a gold padlock and two lines saying the inventory link is off and where to get it. In the transmitter, a small panel titled INVENTORY LINK with an off lamp, one line on what it does and a gold BUY key showing 1 Illyrium coin; once bought the lamp lights and the key becomes the word INSTALLED.

B · Steel shutter

A rolled-down steel shutter covers the inventory: grey slats with a gold-and-black hazard edge and a hanging LOCKED tag. In the transmitter, an empty upgrade socket with the outline of a violet chip and an INSTALL key; once bought the chip sits in the socket and glows.

C · Frosted glass

The inventory stays visible behind frosted blue glass, each slot with a small lock, and a turquoise strip across its top saying the link needs the transmitter upgrade. The player still sees what they carry but cannot touch it. In the transmitter, a coin slot and a lever: PAY puts the Illyrium coin in and throws the lever to LINKED.

Same in every concept

  • Without the upgrade, items move between the machine's own slots, and every button, tab and switch works (on/off, redstone mode, side configuration).
  • With it, the inventory shows normally and items move both ways, as if standing at the machine.
  • An item held on the cursor when the screen closes goes back into the machine; if the machine is full it drops beside the machine and the action bar says so.
  • Only the transmitter's owner can buy the upgrade; team members see whether it is installed. A mined transmitter keeps it.
