Radiation Admin Concepts

Three ways to lay out the admin panel's new RADIATION tab. Each has the new-player protection on top ("Protection ends at": a rank, playtime, or off) and the radiation numbers below it, with SAVE. The panel stays 344 by 210. Seven tabs need narrower tabs (37 pixels) and a short heading, the same in all three.

The example network uses a RankUp ladder of Newcomer, Scout, Sentinel, Guardian, Warden, Champion, with Sentinel chosen. 7 of the 41 players who own placed totems are protected right now. The settings shown are the defaults.

The RADIATION tab

A. Two columns

Protection on the left: three keys (RANK, PLAYTIME, OFF), then the ladder as a list where the chosen rank is lit in violet and the ranks above it are marked as unprotected. With PLAYTIME the list gives way to an hours box with −/+. On the right are the radiation numbers, one row each with −/+. SAVE is at the bottom right, with "Shared by every server" beside it.

Updated with your note: the radiation on/off key sits beside the RADIATION heading. The seven number rows are a little closer together, and under them is B's chart of how far each level is seen. It redraws as you change the numbers; the dotted gold line marks the full-radiation level.

B. A sentence and a ladder

The top reads as a sentence: "Totems give off no radiation until their owner reaches Sentinel", with RANK / PLAYTIME / OFF beside it. Under it the ladder runs left to right as rungs, each with how many totem owners stand on it. Protected rungs are turquoise, and a gold line marks where protection lifts. Below that, the numbers sit in two short columns next to a small chart of how far each level is seen, which redraws as you change them.

C. Three cards

Three cards side by side: PROTECTION (the three keys, the chosen rank with ‹ › to step through the ladder or the hours box, and the protected count), DISTANCE (full level, furthest, blur, with a mini curve) and COATING (cut per layer, most layers, how far out, how often). SAVE runs along the bottom.

The totem's strip while protected (all three)

The bottom strip on the totem's screen while its owner is protected: the level stays, the bar is empty, and the line says what ends the protection. Hovering explains: "New players' totems give off no totem radiation. Yours start at Sentinel." With playtime it reads "until 24 h of playtime (13 h 20 m played)".
