Orb Colour Concepts

The orbs around a placed totem take their colour from a list that covers the first 26 upgrades. Harvest, Sentinel, Extra Homes, Swift Travel and Herald fall off its end, so all five draw plain red, the same as Lockpick. Each option gives those five their own hues; the other 26 stay as they are. Every orb is drawn with the game's own formula (pale at level 1, deep at the top level). The ringed orbs are the five.
