Trade Post Block, Round 2

Three new looks for the Trade Post block, two blocks wide, two tall and one deep. Each has a counter with a small pair of scales on it, the TRADE screen behind them, and front buttons labelled MARKET, SELL, COLLECT and TRADE, the words on the screen you picked. Each look has recessed panels, open spaces and round parts. The grey cube is one block, for scale.

From round 1 (owner, 2026-10-27)

Picked: the main screen C (touch screen with tabs and lot cards), the trade screen C (low and wide), the Market page B (tiles). Block: none of the three.

"Have a small pair of scales on a counter with the screen behind them. Have buttons on the front that are labeled with terms used in the UI. Make them more intricate with more shapes and indents and hollow space."

The three looks

A. Merchant's Counter

Dark oak with brass. The counter front has a raised panel on the left and an open cubby on the right holding a ledger and a coin purse. The four round brass buttons sit in a sunken brass strip with their names engraved under them. The screen has a brass frame with rounded corners and a medallion above it, between two pilasters. There is a recessed toe space at the foot and a service bell and a coin stack on the counter.

B. Exchange Desk

Steel, like the Bank's pillar. The labelled keys are backlit amber in a sunken well on a sloped deck. Below them, an open drawer bay holds a tray that slides out while a trade runs. Under that are bumpers on the corners and a striped kick plate. The screen is set 2 px deep in a vented monitor on a ribbed neck, with a hood and two beacons. The scales hang from an A-frame.

C. Market Kiosk

Cream, like the Cash Machine screens. Two rounded pedestals stand either side of an open, arched nook with a shelf. The labelled beige keys are on a fascia that leans back under a bullnose counter, with a phosphor light line over them. The screen sits deep in a round-topped monitor with a speaker grille, a card slot and a receipt slot.

Built withThe counter, frames, recesses and buttons are a baked model from a generator: cubes, with some turned 22.5°. Round parts (posts, bowls, rims, chains, bumpers, the monitor's round top) and the screen text are drawn by the block entity renderer with MeshDraw's rings, spheres, angled boxes and lines. The button names are part of the block texture. The block faces the player who places it. Admin-placed and unbreakable.
