Resonance System Concepts

Part 1 of the totem energy rework: the chain from a totem to a projector. The energy needs a name, so this page uses Resonance as a working name (the Resonance Crystal is already in the mod and the batteries will be built from it). Every block below drops the word "Totem". 1. How the chain fits together

Totem on top, extractors stacked under it, an interface on an open face of an extractor, a cable from the interface, a repeater in the run, a projector at the end. Pulses show Resonance moving. The projector screen is where the player picks what to send.

Left to right: Shatter Totem, two Resonance Extractors (stacked), a two-block Resonance Interface, Resonance Cable, a Repeater, more cable, a Projector. Pulses are turquoise for energy and gold where a power rides with it.

2. Resonance Extractor

Placed directly under the totem; more can stack below. The totem's output is split evenly between them. Any open face takes an interface. Shown here: two stacked under a totem.

3. Resonance Interface

Touches an open face of an extractor and is where cables plug in. Interfaces that touch join into one, up to twelve blocks in any shape, and carry the energy and powers of every extractor they touch. Shown here: a four-block interface on one extractor.

4. Resonance Repeater

Sits in a cable run and starts a fresh run. No screen of its own: it only sets how much a projector at the end can take. Shown inline with cable on both sides.

5. The Projector screen

The generator's screen moves to the projector: the player chooses what to project here. With repeaters in the run the choice narrows, so the mocks have a repeater count to try. Each is a working mock; click the switches.

A. Three Tabs

POWERS, CHUNKS and STATUS tabs like the Coin Vault. Each tab gets the whole panel; POWERS has the switch list with a column for what arrives after repeaters.

B. Split Panel

Status strip across the top, powers with switches on the left, the chunk map on the right. Everything at once, one screen.

C. Single Column

The current projector screen grown: status, RF, the powers as a switch list instead of chips, chunks underneath. Narrow, so it fits any window.

6. Rules and numbers (proposed)

Totem output

A placed totem makes a base 100 Rs/t at Tier I. A new Base upgrade, Output (sixth path, next to Anchor), raises it: 100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 500 Rs/t over six levels. Extractors split it evenly: two extractors under a Tier I totem get 50 Rs/t each. A projector needs 100 Rs/t to project (the RF it uses today is replaced by Resonance; the RF cap on the projector goes). The batteries and chargers in Part 2 draw from the same supply.

Interface limit

Interfaces that touch join, up to 12. A thirteenth that touches is not part of the interface and shows a red seam. An interface that touches an extractor already served by another interface is refused in the same way, with the first interface to connect keeping the extractor. So an interface cannot be used as a cable: twelve blocks is its longest reach.

Repeaters

Cables carry their full run at full strength: the fall-off along the cable goes. A repeater starts a fresh run of the cable's length. Each repeater between the interface and a projector costs the projector a choice:

RepeatersSend one powerSend two or more
0Full levelFull level each, up to the channel count
1Full levelEach power one level lower
2Full levelEach power two levels lower
3Full levelEach power three levels lower; a power at level 3 or below cannot be sent this way

Energy itself is never reduced by a repeater. A repeater with no projector after it only extends the run. The projector screen shows the arriving level for whatever is chosen, so the player sees the cost before switching.

What a projector can choose from

Everything the interface carries: the powers of every totem on an extractor it touches, and, in Part 2, the powers stored in batteries on extractors. Chunk loading (Anchor) is one of the powers and is chosen on the projector like the others. The number of powers one projector sends at once stays at two, plus one with a Channel Expander fitted in the projector.
