Totem Radiation Concepts

Three concepts each for the radiation readout on the totem's screen, the coated look of the shielding panel, window and door, and the Totem Radiation Coating item. They follow your answers. The number is the radiation level (tier plus upgrades). The bar is how far away a compass can see the totem, out of 2,500 blocks, after coating; the part the coating takes away shows dim. The info button sits beside it.

The example is your own Tier IV totem with 59 upgrade levels, so radiation level 63, seen from 689 blocks. It is inside 2 coated layers, so a compass sees it from 441 blocks.

1. The readout on the totem's screen

The totem's screen is 326 by 261: the upgrade tree on the left, the detail panel on the right, the coins along the top and the owner lines along the bottom. The tree and detail panel are sketched here; only the readout is drawn exactly.

A. In the bottom-right corner

The readout sits where a thief sees the STEAL button (the owner never does): RADIATION and the number on one line with the info button, the bar under it, then "Seen up to 441 blocks" and "2 coated layers". The screen keeps its size.

B. A full-width strip along the bottom

The screen grows 24 pixels taller for a strip under everything else. The long bar is marked every 500 blocks, so the distance reads like a ruler. A gold notch marks 441, and the dim stretch runs on to 689.

C. A gauge on the side

A narrow column fixed to the right edge of the screen, like a thermometer: RAD and the number at the top, a bar that fills from the bottom, and the info button at the foot. It is visible from every tab.

The info button (all three)

Clicking i shows your sentence in a box beside it until you click again or move away. Hovering over the bar says how far the totem is seen from with and without the coating.

2. Coated shielding blocks

Each view shows the same small wall. On the left are two plain panels; in the middle a coated panel with a coated window above it; on the right a coated door. The base pictures are the mod's own textures. The coating is drawn over them per block, so joined panels keep joining as today.

A. A turquoise glaze

The whole face takes a thin Illyrium-turquoise glaze. A bright top and left edge make it look wet and glossy, and the rivets turn turquoise. The glass on the window gets a faint tint.

B. Sealed seams

The face stays lead grey. A turquoise bead of sealant runs round each block's edge, and a small Illyrium hex stamp sits in the middle. Reads like a sealed, inspected panel.

C. A woven mesh

A fine turquoise lattice is laid over the face like a screen. On the window it reads as wire mesh in the glass, and on the door it covers both halves.

3. The Totem Radiation Coating item

Each picture shows the item in a hotbar beside the three blocks it coats, then the item at eight times its size. The recipe makes 4 from 1 Pure Illyrium Dust, 4 Lead Plates and 4 Tetrium Dust.

A. A tin of coating

A lead-grey tin with a turquoise band and a drip running down from the lid.

B. A sealed flask

A glass flask of glowing turquoise compound with a lead stopper.

C. A trowel and compound

A trowel with a blob of turquoise compound on its blade, ready to spread.
