VIP Board Concepts

A new board for the Score Screen that shows every VIP as a head tile with their name underneath. An admin's message sits at the top, a second message and the support link at the bottom; only the tiles between them scroll. Each concept is drawn on a 4 by 3 screen at the Score Screen's own pixel size, with made-up players and faces. Everything here is the screen's renderer: the Score Screen block itself does not change.

What the pictures assume (the defaults of the questions in chat)

  • VIPs: everyone in the LuckPerms group vip, online or not, the same on every server.
  • Order: alphabetical.
  • The texts and link are typed in each Score Screen's own settings (section 3). &6 turns the top message gold.
  • The link opens Minecraft's own "open this website?" prompt when a player right-clicks it on the screen (section 4).

1. Three looks on a 4 by 3 screen

A. Wall of heads

The board's usual turquoise title strip ("OUR VIPS"), the top message under it in up to two lines. The middle is a grid of dark tiles, a face with its hat layer and the name under it, rising slowly and fading out under the top message and above the bottom strip. When everyone fits the grid stands still, centred. At the foot a strip with the second message and the address, underlined with a pointer.

B. Marquee

The top message takes the title's place on a purple banner with gold edges, in the VIP tag's colours (no message: "OUR VIPS"). Two rows of heads in gold-edged squares slide sideways like a film strip, the lower row half a tile behind. At the foot the message at the left and a SUPPORT key at the right, with the address small under it.

C. Portrait gallery

A dark velvet wall. The top message on a gold ribbon with notched ends. Each VIP is a portrait in a bevelled gold frame with their name on a brass plate. A row holds for a few seconds, then the wall glides up by one row, like a lift. At the foot the message and the address in gold, with "RIGHT-CLICK TO OPEN" beside it.

2. The same looks on a small 2 by 2 screen

On a small screen the messages drop to one line each and shrink to fit, and the tiles get smaller. Under 2 by 2 there is no room for tiles; the screen shows the two messages and the link only.

3. Where an admin types the texts

The Score Screen settings (an admin right-clicks the screen). With SHOW on BOARD and the VIPs board picked, the rows that do nothing for this board (look, rotation, layout) give their place to three boxes. In a rotation that includes the VIPs board, a VIP TEXTS key opens the same three boxes. The link is refused unless it starts with http:// or https://.

Settings with the VIPs board

TOP and BOTTOM take up to 80 characters each; LINK up to 200. Every change shows on the screen in the world at once, as the other settings do.

The other way (question VB3, option b)

Typed once for the whole network with /bsp vips message top|bottom|link <text> (or a page in the admin panel), so every VIP board on every server shows the same words. The screen's settings only pick the board.

4. What a player sees when they click the link

There is no separate window for players: the Score Screen is only a block in the world, so "clicking" is a right-click on the screen. The game works out where on the face the click landed.

Default: Minecraft's own link prompt

A right-click on the link line opens the game's standard prompt with the full address: YES opens the browser, COPY TO CLIPBOARD copies it, NO closes it. A right-click anywhere else on the board does nothing for players; admins get the settings there as now.

Option: a line in chat

[BSP] Support the server: shop.buildsecureprotect.com/products/vip

A right-click anywhere on the VIPs board puts one clickable line in that player's chat (clicking it opens the same prompt). Easier to hit from a distance, but it adds to chat.
