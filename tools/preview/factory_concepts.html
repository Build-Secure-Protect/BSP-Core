Joined machine

Glass only on the two outer ends. Between slices the walls are gone: one roof, one tray, one drive shaft with a glowing coupling at every join, and the podium screen centred on the front.

Single slice

Glass on both sides, the screen on the front block, machinery under every belt: motor and flywheel at the back, press anvil and gear train in the middle, drive rollers at the front.

Motivators

Half a block high. Neighbours link with a conduit at the base and an energy beam between cores, along a slice and across slices. More Motivators on a slice means a faster pulse.

Your eight notes

  • 1. Glass sides: framed glass panes on every outside wall, plus a glass strip in the roof.
  • 2. Machinery: motor, flywheel, gear train, press anvil, pumps, pipes and a drive shaft fill the space under the belts.
  • 3. Joined slices: inner walls drop away, slices share one rib, and the shaft, roof rail and tray run straight through with a coupling at each join.
  • 4. Motivator height: half a block.
  • 5. Motivator links: base conduits and core beams join neighbours in both directions.
  • 6. Coin colour: the blank and the coin on the belt follow the tier being pressed. Use the Coin buttons.
  • 7. Coin models: the belt, die and tray show the mod's own blank and Shatter Coin item sprites.
  • 8. Detail: plating, vents, bolts, rollers, chutes, hydraulic lines and lamps throughout.
