Shop Card Limits

Spawn shops task 1 (SS-2 sell limits, SS-4 per-player refills). Three ways for a shop card to show how much a player can still buy or sell before the limit refills. Each look is drawn on the real Cash Machine shop screen at its in-game size: the weekly Challenge shop (4 cards on show, sell only, 4 lots each per player a week) and a Decor page at 7 cards a row (some cards limited to 16 lots a day per player). Change the state to see the counts move.

A. Count in the corner

The card's top-right corner shows what is left of the limit as a fraction, 3/4: green when it is how many you can still buy, amber when it is how many the shop will still take from you. At zero it reads OUT (nothing left to buy) or FULL (the shop takes no more) in red, and the card goes dark. Today's corner number for stock becomes the same fraction.

Decor, 7 cards a row

B. Gauge on the bottom edge

No new text on the card. Its bottom edge becomes a 2-pixel gauge that empties from right to left as the limit is used: green for what you can still buy, amber for what the shop will still take. A used-up limit leaves a short red stub and the card goes dark. Today's stock number stays in the corner; the exact counts and the refill time are in the tooltip.

Decor, 7 cards a row

C. Price line that flips

The price line takes turns, like a cash machine display: 3 seconds of the price (sell 2,500), then 1.5 seconds of the count (3 of 4 left). At zero the line stays on full or sold out in red and the card goes dark. Cards with no limit never flip. With reduced motion turned on, the count shows on its own line in place of the empty "--".

The same in every look

  • The tooltip gives the full sentence: "You can sell 1 more of 4. Refills Monday 00:00 (in 2d 4h)", and for a shop-wide limit "The shop takes 182 more from everyone".
  • A sale that runs into the limit sells what is left and says so in the message line: "Sold 1 lot for 2,500 pieces. The shop takes no more Steel Casing from you this week; the rest is still in your inventory."
  • Sell all and Sell here stop at the limits and hand back the rest.
  • The quantity screen's Max also counts the limit left.
