Kits: screen concepts

Two sets, three looks each. Set 1 is the KITS tab on the admin panel (violet, the operator palette): the kit list and the editor an admin reaches through EDIT or NEW. Set 2 is the player's kit browser (teal). Everything is drawn at one and a half times the in-game size. Item icons are stand-ins. Pick one per set; combining is fine ("editor B with A's settings rows").

Set 1: the KITS tab and the editor (admin panel)

The admin panel is 344 x 210 today with PLAYERS, SEASON and SETTINGS. A fourth tab KITS lists the kits; EDIT or NEW turns the panel into the editor. All three looks grow the panel to 344 x 290 while the editor is open and shrink back on CLOSE. /bsp admin kits opens the panel on the tab.

Show:

A. Workbench (default)

The kit's 36 slots top left, the item browser to their right with a search line and a wheel-scrolled grid, the settings as compact rows under the slots, keys along the bottom. One screen, nothing hidden.

Picks for set 1

  1. Look: A workbench, B pages, or C three columns (or a mix).
  2. The list: rows with columns (A), a list with a detail pane (B), or cards (C).
  3. Mod filter chips over the browser (B shows them): yes or no.
  4. Rank picker: chips of the group names (A), or RankUp's ladder drawn as steps (C) with chips as the fallback when RankUp is absent.
  5. TEST ON ME (gives the admin the kit at once, no record): keep or drop.
  6. GIVE TO… on the list (pick an online player, the kit becomes owed to them): keep or drop.

Set 2: the player's kit browser

Opened by the KITS button above the inventory, a key binding or /kits. Teal frame like the other player screens. Each kit shows one of: READY, a cooldown left, the rank to reach, the server to be on, the price, the slots short, WAITING (owed: a reward or grant that did not fit yet), or REDEEMED greyed out until it is ready again. Drawn with the player at rank member, 620 pieces in the bank, 3 free slots.

A. Catalogue (default)

The kits in rows on the left with icon, name and a status chip; the chosen kit on the right: its items in the 9-wide grid, the description, the requirement line and one key, REDEEM or the reason in its place. Redeemed rows are greyed.

B. Card wall

Every kit as a card with a large icon, its name and its status on the card itself: a thin cooldown bar draining, the rank step, the price, or a REDEEM key right on a ready card. No second pane; a click on a card flips it to its items. Redeemed cards sit grey and bloom back when ready.

Picks for set 2

  1. Look: A catalogue, B card wall, or C rail (or a mix: "B's cards with A's detail pane").
  2. Requirement story: one line plus the ladder (A), on the card (B), or the ticked list of steps (C).
  3. The ladder: RankUp's ladder as steps with "you" and the needed rank marked (all three show it); when RankUp is absent the line names the group only.
  4. Redeemed look: greyed icon and name with "in 6 h 12 m" (A), the draining bar (B), or the rail's small time (C).
  5. The redeem key: one big key at the bottom that follows the chosen kit (A, C), or on the card (B).
  6. Owed kits: shown in violet as WAITING with TAKE IT (all three): keep, or teal like the rest.
  7. Header line (rank, pieces, free slots): keep or drop.
