BSP-Core Reference Sheet

26 plates: every machine with its build layout and finished look, the totem extras, every block and item, every recipe, and how each metal and coin is made. Each plate is also saved as an image for CurseForge.

Single-block machines

BSP-Core

Tetrium Crucible

Smelts one Tetrium Ore into 2 Tetrium Nuggets and 1 Tetrium Slag in 20 seconds. Burns furnace fuel.

Combination Forge

Presses nine nuggets into an ingot (Tetrium, Magnatite, and Illyrium with the Illyrium Forge Upgrade) and Tetrium Ingots into Tetrium Plates. Runs on fuel, or on RF with an RF Upgrade.

Illyrium Crucible (3 x 3 x 3)

BSP-Core

Build it

Each grid is one layer seen from above. The bottom row of a grid is the front of the machine.

  • 12 x Illyrium Casing
  • 8 x Lava Pylon
  • 2 x Item Hatch
  • 1 x Illyrium Core
  • 1 x Illyrium Crucible Controller

Finished

Runs on lava. Smelts Illyrium Ore with slag into Dirty Illyrium Ingots, and alloys Pure Illyrium Dust with Tetrium Dust into Illyrium Nuggets. Lower pylons take lava, upper pylons take RF.

Sneak + right-click the controller in game for the step-by-step Assembly Guide.

Illyrium Refinery

BSP-Core

Build it

Each grid is one layer seen from above. The bottom row of a grid is the front of the machine.

  • 8 x Illyrium Casing
  • 1 x Item Hatch
  • 1 x Illyrium Core
  • 2 x Illyrium Tank Glass
  • 1 x Refinery Pump
  • 1 x Illyrium Refinery Controller

Finished

Washes Dirty Illyrium Dust into Pure Illyrium Dust with water and a filter. The pump takes water and RF; the Item Hatch takes dust in and pure dust out.

Sneak + right-click the controller in game for the step-by-step Assembly Guide.

Shatter Coin Factory (one slice: 1 x 2 x 3)

BSP-Core

Build it

Each grid is one layer seen from above. The bottom row of a grid is the front of the machine.

  • 1 x Factory Blank Hatch
  • 2 x Factory Frame
  • 1 x Shatter Coin Factory Controller
  • 1 x Factory Power Port
  • 1 x Factory Press

Mo Up to three Motivators on top of a slice cut its press time by 15%, 30% and 50%.

Finished

Presses coin blanks into Shatter Coins in real time, from 12 hours for Copper to 7 days for Illyrium. Needs RF. Up to 10 slices side by side join into one machine.

Sneak + right-click the controller in game for the step-by-step Assembly Guide.

Magnetic Centrifuge (1 x 3 x 3, stackable)

BSP-Core

Build it

Each grid is one layer seen from above. The bottom row of a grid is the front of the machine.

  • 13 x Centrifuge Casing
  • 2 x Centrifuge Rotor
  • 1 x Centrifuge Power Port
  • 1 x Item Hatch
  • 1 x Magnetic Centrifuge Controller

Finished

Always needs RF. Separates Magnatite Ore into nuggets and Carbon Dust, and with a Copper Tetrium Coil charges Magnatite Ingots. More layers give more nuggets and a better charge chance.

Sneak + right-click the controller in game for the step-by-step Assembly Guide.

Totem Generators and the Totem Projector

BSP-Core

Generators under a totem

One to nine generators in a three by three with the totem on the middle one. They join into one slab, with three cable ports on every outer edge. A ring rises around the totem while they have RF.

Totem Projector

Recreates two of the totem's base powers as a second aura, wherever a Totem Cable from a generator reaches it.

CableLongest run
Ca Tetrium Core Totem Cable15 blocks
Ca Magnatite Core Totem Cable25 blocks
Ca Illyrium Core Totem Cable40 blocks
Ca Charged Illyrium Core Totem Cable80 blocks
A generator pushes 15 blocks, or 25, 40 and 80 with Reach Amplifiers. Strength falls from full to half along the run.

Decoy Totem

BSP-Core

What its owner sees

The Hollow Idol on its Decoy Power Base, with a faint ghost of the totem around it.

What everyone else sees

An ordinary Shatter Totem. Rival Totem Compasses within range point at it. Try to steal it and its traps go off.

Needs 100 RF per tick. Five per player. Sockets: Range Coils, trap charges, a Trap Amplifier, Reinforced Casings.

Coin Vault and Anti Totem Block

BSP-Core

Coin Vault, joined

Vault blocks of one owner join into one vault of up to 3 x 3 x 3. Coins inside earn interest. Others can pick the lock for a quarter of the coins.

Anti Totem Block (admins)

Keeps Shatter Totems and chosen BSP blocks out of a box of up to 256 blocks in each direction. For spawn areas, hubs and arenas.
