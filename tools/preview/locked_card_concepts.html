Locked Shop Cards

Spawn shops task 7 (SS-8). A card (or a whole shop) can need a permission (a rank) or some hours of play. When it is set to show while locked, the player sees it but cannot buy it; set to hide, it is simply not there. Three looks for a locked card, on an Advanced shop page: Fusion Reactor Controller needs the Gold rank, Antiprotonic Nucleosynthesizer needs 10 hours of play. The tooltip is open on the first.

A. Dimmed, with a padlock

The card goes dark like a sold-out one, the item drawn faint, a small padlock in the top-left corner and its price line replaced by locked in red. The tooltip says why, in red, with the player's progress for playtime.

B. Price kept, LOCK badge

The card keeps its colours and price, so players see what is waiting for them; the item is drawn grey and a red LOCK badge takes the corner where the limit count goes. The tooltip says why.

C. The requirement on the card

The card is dimmed and its price line is replaced by the requirement in short, in amber: Gold rank, 10 h play. The player reads what they need without hovering; the tooltip has the details and the price.
