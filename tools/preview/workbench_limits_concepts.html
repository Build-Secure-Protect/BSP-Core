Workbench Limit Rows

Where the shop Workbench sets a card's limits: stock and sell room, each for everyone and per player, and the four refill rules. The side panel is already full (ten rows), so each look finds room a different way. Drawn at the Workbench's real size with the Challenge shop open and the Steel Casing card selected (sell 2,500; the shop takes 5 from everyone and 3 from each player; refills every Monday at 00:00).

State

A. Two tabs on the side panel

The side panel's heading becomes two tabs, CARD and LIMITS. CARD keeps the lot size, prices, currency, instant, hidden and section. LIMITS lists a BUY group and a SELL group, each with everyone, each player, refill and player refill, and the shop-wide count left beside the limit. Click a row to edit it in the field at the foot of the panel, as every row works today. The page of cards stays in view.

B. One list that scrolls

The side panel keeps a single list of every row, under the headings CARD, BUY LIMITS and SELL LIMITS, and scrolls with the mouse wheel. A thin bar on the panel's right edge shows where you are. Nothing else on the screen changes; here it is scrolled down to the sell limits.

C. A limits table on the main screen

A Limits key, in its own group in the key column, swaps the page of cards for a table of the selected card's limits: Buy and Sell columns, with rows for everyone, each player, both refill rules and what is left now. Click a cell, then type, or pick one of the rule buttons under the table (never, 1h, 12h, day 00:00, mon 00:00, rotation, same). Back returns to the page. The side panel stays as the card's own rows.
