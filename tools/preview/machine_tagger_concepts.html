Machine Tagger

The hand-held tool that tags machines for the Holo Grid. Right-click a machine to tag it, sneak-right-click to untag it, and right-click the air to open its screen. Two things to pick: how the item looks, and how its screen is laid out. Every screen has the same parts: the tracked machines with their names, renaming, removing a tag, buying tag slots with Copper Shatter Coins, and the Remote Control unlock for 4 Diamond Shatter Coins.

Drag a model to turn it. In the screens, click the pencil, type a new name and press Enter. BUY and UNLOCK spend the example coins.

1. The item

An item model about one block tall, held in the hand. Slots belong to the player, not the item, so a new tagger shows the same count.

A. Scanner Gun

A pistol grip with rubber rings, a steel body with vent slats and a turquoise seam, and a barrel ending in a glowing lens inside a steel ring. A small screen on top shows 6/8 and eight tag pips. A Copper coin sits half into a slot on the side, by three status lights. A short antenna with a green tip blinks while the tagger is held.

B. Tag Tablet

A handheld tablet with orange rubber corners and a seam. Its screen lists the tagged machines. At the top edge, a tag printer pushes out a paper tag with a green stripe. A steel stylus clips to the side, a leather strap runs down the back, and a scanner lens sits in a steel ring at the back.

C. Tag Stamper

A round stamp with a glowing turquoise face and a ring of ten green lamps that chase round it. The knurled rubber grip ends in a steel knob with a turquoise band. A small counter window on the head reads 6/8. An orange tag reel turns on the side, feeding paper down to the head, and a Copper coin sits in a slot on the neck.

2. The screen

The screen opens when you right-click the air with the tagger. It shows the server, the world and the distance, but never coordinates, so streamers can show it.

A. Ledger

One row per slot, numbered: lamp, name, machine type and where it is, with RENAME (pencil) and REMOVE (red cross) at the end of the row. Empty slots say how to fill them, and locked slots show a padlock. At the bottom are two cards, set apart. TAG SLOTS shows twenty pips (gold free, turquoise bought), the next slot's price in Copper and BUY SLOT. REMOTE CONTROL has a violet padlock, the four Diamond coins and UNLOCK.

B. Device Screen

The screen looks like the tagger's own glass in a steel casing. Three round keys on the casing choose the page: TAGS (two columns of cards; click one for its rename and remove keys), SLOTS (all twenty slots with their prices; click the next one to buy it) and REMOTE (a big padlock with four sockets that fill with your Diamond coins). On the right of the casing are a speaker grille, a slot with a Copper coin bobbing in it, and the remote lamp.

C. Tag Cards

Each tagged machine is a paper luggage tag pinned to a corkboard, with a green stripe that turns grey while the machine is asleep. Empty slots are dashed outlines. Locked slots are grey tags, and the next one carries its price: click it to buy. Pick a tag and its name shows on an orange label maker below, with RENAME and UNTAG. A steel plate on the right holds the Remote Control padlock and UNLOCK.

What every concept shares

PartBehaviour
TaggingRight-click a machine you could open yourself: in your own claim, a claim you are a member of, or unclaimed land, and never another player's private Mekanism machine. The action bar says "Tagged Elite Smelting Factory (7 of 8)". When every slot is full, it says so and names the price of the next slot.
UntaggingSneak-right-click the machine, or use REMOVE in the screen. The slot is free again. When a tagged machine is broken, its tag is removed and you are told.
Slots2 free. Each extra slot costs 8 more Copper coins than the one before (8, 16, 24 and so on), up to 20 slots. Coins come from your inventory. All of it is set in the config.
Remote ControlA one-time unlock for 4 Diamond coins. It lets you operate a machine from the Holo Grid while you stand inside a Remote Control Transmitter's range.
ResetsA player reset or the end of a season takes the bought slots back to the 2 free ones, removes every tag and locks Remote Control again. Layouts and presets stay.
NamesUp to 24 characters. A name is yours only: team members tag and name their own.
