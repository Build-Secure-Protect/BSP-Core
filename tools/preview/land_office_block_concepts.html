Land Office Looks

Three designs for the Land Office block: one block, placed by admins, right-click opens the land map where players buy plots from Region Guard. All three belong to the bank family (dark steel, amber lamps, gold trim, a green phosphor surface) so an office sits well beside a Shatter Bank. The screen is already built; only the block's look is open.

Drag a view to turn it.

2. What each is made of

A. Map CounterB. Surveyor's CabinetC. Deeds Board
BodyA dark steel counter on a bolted base, the Vault Pillar's materials. A slanted map table on top under a glass lid: a green phosphor map with amber plot squares, a brass stamp and an ink pad at the corner, two rolled maps in a rack on the side.A walnut cabinet with brass corners and three map drawers down the front, a slanted lid holding a lit plan under glass, a brass theodolite on a tripod on top, a compass rose dial on the side.A notice board on two steel posts: a green phosphor board with the land map and three amber deed cards pinned to it, a narrow counter shelf in front with a bell and a stamp, an amber lamp on an arm above.
Glows at nightThe map table, the plot squares, the lamp on the stamp post.The plan under the lid, the theodolite's eyepiece, the dial.The board, the deed cards, the lamp.
MovesPlot squares blink in turn while someone uses it; the stamp lifts and drops.The theodolite turns slowly; the plan's cursor sweeps.The deed cards flutter; the lamp pulses while someone uses it.
Reads asThe bank's land desk. Closest to the ATM and the deposit box.A surveyor's office. Most "map".A town hall notice board. Lightest, easiest to place in a market.
Built withOne block, admin-only item, a static model from a generator (`gen_bank_assets.py`, the machine atlas) with the glowing parts drawn full bright, and a small renderer for what moves. Light level 5 while whole, like the stall displays.

3. Decisions, with defaults

  1. Look. A, B or C. Default A, Map Counter, since it matches the bank blocks it stands beside.
  2. Light. Default 5 at night, like the displays; the ATM gives 7.
  3. Idle motion. Default: the plot squares blink only while someone has the screen open; still otherwise.
  4. Sound. Default: the bank's key beep on opening.
