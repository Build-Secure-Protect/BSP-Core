Bank & Market admin page

Three layouts for the admin panel's new page. Each shows where the BANK & MARKET button sits on the Players tab, the page itself (coins, pieces, the bank log, EDIT TRANSACTIONS, and the player's market lots and requests), and the larger page that refunds transactions. The mocks are drawn at twice the panel's in-game pixel size. Click a REFUND button to see the second-click confirm. The player, rows and lots are examples.

What all three share

  • Moderators can open every page and read it; REFUND is greyed for them, as with the panel's other actions.
  • Coins shown are the ones kept in the bank account. The Coin Vault's coins stay on the Players tab.
  • Rows where the player gained (a sale, a payment received, a daily reward) have no REFUND. A row refunds once.
  • A refund writes its own REFUND row into the player's log, and the player gets a chat line only they see, at once or at their next login.
  • On a network, the page reads the log and lots from the database, so it shows rows from every server.
