Wireframe: three looks

Wireframe is the Mob Chamber power made from many spider eyes. While you stand in a Projector's reach, every outsider in that reach is drawn for you through walls. A raider with Shroud on the totem in their offhand stays hidden for Shroud's seconds after stepping in. Each view below shows a raider walking behind a stone wall, with the Projector and you (the owner) on the near side. The pink is the Wireframe pool's colour. Drag a view to turn it.

How the raider is drawn

Your pick

1. The look. (a) Every body part outlined in lines, the raider's limbs swinging as they walk: the wireframe you described (default). (b) Only the outline of their hitbox, with their name and distance above it, as Sentinel's markers show intruders. (c) A faint filled shape with bright edges and a pulsing ring at their feet, the easiest to see from far off.

2. Who sees it. (a) Everyone on the projector's team who stands in reach: the owner, their team, Ward friends and allies with the Ward perk (default). (b) Only the owner and players with Upgrades access.

Built already, whatever the look: the server works out who sees whom once a second (GameTested), and Shroud's delay restarts if the raider steps out and back in.
