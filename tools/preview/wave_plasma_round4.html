Wave Plasma Round 4

Your Round 3 picks applied (Header Rack interface, Capsule batteries, Cartridge cells). Two redesigns: the Battery Charger with the item sitting inside it, in the spirit of the AE2 Charger, and the Wave Emitter as a tricorder with a slot for the cartridge. Then the next group: totem access permissions, and the aura as a cube with a way to hide it. Drag any 3D view to turn it.

1. Battery Charger, redesigned

Open designs where you see the battery or cell held inside and filling. Each is shown with a Capsule battery in it; the cartridge cell sits in the same cradle. The cable comes in from the left.

2. Wave Emitter, as a tricorder

A handheld with a display, held in the offhand like the totem, and a slot the Cartridge cell clicks into. Three takes; each is shown with a cell fitted and the lid open where there is one.

3. Totem access: letting friends in

A new ACCESS tab on the totem panel. Each person added gets two switches: Upgrade (may buy upgrades and change chunks and projector settings) and Trusted (does not set off Alarm, is not weakened by Ward, may open the totem's extractors, interfaces, projectors and chargers). Three ways to lay it out; each is a working mock.

A. One List

Like the Coin Vault's access list: a name box, Add, and one row per player with the two switches. Up to 8 players.

B. Two Columns

UPGRADERS on the left, TRUSTED on the right; a player can be in both. Each column has its own Add. Reads as two separate permissions.

C. Access Cards

Nine card slots. You craft a Totem Access Card, right-click the totem with it to bind it to a player's name, and drop it in a slot; the card's row gets the two switches. Take the card out and the access goes with it. Cards can be handed over or stolen.

Rules (proposed)

Only the owner edits the list; it is kept when the totem is picked up and cleared when it is stolen. Access reaches everything on the totem's plasma chain and its projectors' chunk pickers. Trusted players are still not owners: they cannot steal-protect, cannot pick the totem up, and do not get the Carried powers. An admin sees and may clear any list.

4. The aura as a cube

Auras become cubes: a power with radius r covers the cube from r blocks west to r blocks east, down and up, north and south of the totem, so a radius-5 Fortify is an 11 x 11 x 11 box that players can measure and build to. Projectors get the same. Three looks for the cube, each on a totem with two powers (Fortify 5, Healing 8).

Hiding the auras

Two places, both proposed: a client setting Show totem auras (in the mod's config screen, and a keybind defaulting to unbound) that hides every aura for that player only; and a switch on the totem's panel header, AURAS: SHOWN / HIDDEN, which does the same thing from inside the game. Hiding is per player: it never changes what the aura does, and other players still see it unless they hide it too.
