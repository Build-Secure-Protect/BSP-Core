Factory Plasma Concepts

Where Wave Plasma enters a Shatter Coin Factory slice, and how much faster it presses. A slice is 1 wide, 2 high, 3 long: controller, Frame, Blank Hatch along the bottom; Frame, Press, Power Port along the top; up to three Motivators on top of that. In a joined machine the slices stand side by side, so the only faces that stay free are the back (hatch and port), the front (controller and top Frame) and the top row. Drag any 3D view to turn it; the slider drives every view and screen.

Decided (2026-10-10) and built

B, the Plasma Injector, in a Motivator cell; UI-1, a PLASMA gauge beside the RF gauge; curve x2 at 300 mB/t, x3 at 500, straight lines between, flat above (plasma.injectorRates / plasma.injectorFactors); RF stays required; only slices with their own injector speed up; a cable connects only through the port on the far end. The same block also serves every other BSP machine and other mods' machines. The built look is the Nozzle from the injector mock-ups: bolted foot plate, stepped nozzle and four feed tubes into the machine, glass barrel with the plasma level, bolted cap, port ring.

1. Where the plasma goes in

Yellow ring = RF socket, blue square = item hatch, ion-blue ring = plasma socket (same colour as the interface and the charger). The plasma cable is drawn where it would really have to sit.

2. The screen

The factory screen today: one lane per slice with a progress bar and Motivator pips, an RF gauge below. Three ways to show the plasma.

UI-1. Second gauge

A plasma gauge in ion blue next to the RF gauge, with the arriving rate and the speed it buys. Lanes unchanged. Plainest.

UI-2. Speed line

One SPEED line under the lanes: the combined factor from Motivators, plasma and Overclock, and what each contributes. The gauge only shows the tank.

UI-3. Pips per lane

Each lane gets blue pips under its violet Motivator pips, one per 100 mB/t that lane is getting, so a half-fed machine is visible lane by lane. Busiest.

4. Rules

RuleProposed
What arrives countsThe factory is a plasma receiver like a base or a charger: the interface feeds it once a second with what the run delivers. Speed comes from the rate arriving, not from a stored amount, so a cut cable or a stolen totem drops the slice back to normal speed within a second. Chunks must be loaded for plasma to arrive (Anchor); presses still run offline, at the unboosted speed.
Per sliceDecided: only a slice with its own injector speeds up, by what arrives at that injector. Ten slices at full speed want ten injectors and 5,000 mB/t. Nothing is pooled.
CableDecided: a cable connects only through the port on the end away from the machine; cables on the sides neither connect nor feed.
CurveDecided: x2 at 300 mB/t and x3 at 500, straight lines from x1 at 0 through each point, flat after the last (plasma.injectorRates = [300, 500], plasma.injectorFactors = [2.0, 3.0]). One curve for every machine, the factory included.
StackingMultiplies with Motivators and Overclock: time = press hours x (1 - Motivator cut) / plasma factor / Overclock. Three Motivators and 200 mB/t make a 96 h Illyrium coin a 24 h one.
EnergyDecided: RF stays required everywhere; plasma only speeds. (Option A's plasma-pays-for-the-press idea was not chosen.)
Screen and JadeThe arriving rate and the factor show on the screen; Jade shows "Plasma 200 mB/t, x2.0" on any block of the slice; the interface's flow view lists the factory like a base.
