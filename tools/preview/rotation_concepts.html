Rotation Announcements

Spawn shops task 3 (SS-6). Two looks to pick before code: the chat line every player gets when a rotating shop changes its cards, and the Workbench's Settings view for a rotating shop, which needs room for three new rows (rotate at, no repeats, announce). The example is the weekly Challenge shop changing on Monday at 00:00.

The chat line

A. One sentence

A gold [Shops] tag, then a plain sentence naming the shop and its new cards in aqua, and the next change in grey. Hover a card name for the item's tooltip. It runs to two lines in a normal chat width.

B. Headline and list

Three short lines: a gold headline, CHALLENGE: NEW CARDS, then the cards in aqua separated by dots, then the next change in grey. It stands out from player chat and is easy to scan.

C. Item links

Green shop name, then each card in square brackets as vanilla shows a linked item, coloured by its rarity, and the next change in dark grey. The shortest of the three, and it looks like the item links players already know.

The Workbench's Settings for a rotating shop

A. One column, grouped

Only the rows for the shop's kind, in one column with a thin rule between the groups: the shop, rotation, opening hours. Rows sit a pixel closer than today (nine instead of ten) so fourteen fit above the hint.

B. Two columns

The shop's own rows on the left (name, kind, parent, network, buy only, id), the rotation rows and the opening hours on the right, each under a small heading, in three-quarter text.

C. Three tabs

Tabs across the top of Settings, SHOP, ROTATION and HOURS, like the side panel's CARD and LIMITS tabs. Each holds a few rows at today's size and spacing. Shown here on ROTATION.

The card's Weight row (the same with every look)

On a rotating shop, the side panel's CARD tab gains Weight under Opens section: 0 never drawn, 1 by default, up to 100.
