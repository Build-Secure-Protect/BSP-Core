BSP-Core · Totem radiation detected

While tracking, a rival totem's radiation reaches you but the totem is outside your compass's reach. The needle points nowhere and turns slowly, and a popup says Totem radiation detected. Three looks for the popup follow; the needle and the compass screen are the same in all three.

Look A

The words sit above the hotbar in gold, where the game puts its own short messages, and fade unless the compass keeps them up.

Look B

A dark chip above the hotbar, edged in gold like the Sentinel strip's chips, with the compass turning on its left and a slow gold pulse on its right.

Look C

A toast in the top right, where advancements arrive, in the mod's turquoise frame, with a second line saying the totem is not in your reach yet.

In every look

The needle: today's wobble with nothing found, and the steady turn, once every 4 seconds, when radiation is detected.

The compass screen says the same words in gold where it says "No rival totem within range" today.

The popup shows only while the compass is in your hand, and gives no strength, count or distance. A rival totem in your reach still wins: the needle points at it as it does today.
