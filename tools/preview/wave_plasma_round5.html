Round 4 picks applied: the Slab emitter with the cartridge the other way up (contacts inside), and the ACCESS list with four separate switches. Then the last group: the new effects (Cloaking, Bouncy, Recall, Recall Block, X-ray), where they sit on the tree, their numbers, and how they look. Drag any 3D view to turn it.

1. Applied from Round 4

Wave Emitter, Slab, cartridge turned

The cartridge now goes in contacts-first: the copper connections sit inside the emitter and the tier cap shows at the bottom edge.

ACCESS tab, four switches

Per player: Upgrades (buy upgrades, change chunks and projector settings), Alarm (does not set it off), Ward (not weakened by it), Machines (may open the totem's extractors, interfaces, projectors and chargers). Click the switches.

3. Cloaking

The aura cube shows outsiders the land as it was, not what is inside. Shown: a base inside a cloak cube seen from outside, then the same from inside. Click to switch.

Outside: the cube renders the saved terrain; nothing inside is drawn.

How it would work. When Cloaking is first switched on, the server saves a copy of the natural terrain in the cube: it generates the chunks again from the world seed into a scratch copy and keeps the blocks inside the cube (so what is saved is the land as generated, not the base). Players outside the cube who are not on the access list see that copy drawn in place of the real blocks; from inside, or for trusted players, everything is normal. Entities inside are hidden from outside too. Cloaking draws 50 mB/t of plasma from the totem while on, so a cloaked base needs Output. Levels raise the cube: radius 6, 9, 12, 16.

5. Recall

When your totem is being stolen, a prompt offers to bring you back near it. Three prompt designs; each counts down. Click Accept or Decline.

A. Banner

A bar across the top of the screen with the countdown, like the steal HUD.

B. Compass Card

A small card above the hotbar with an arrow toward the totem, its distance, and the two keys.

C. Centre Prompt

A box in the middle of the screen that cannot be missed, with a thin ring draining as time runs out.

From another server it hands over to BSP-Teleport. Declining costs nothing.

Every number above goes in the config under effects, as per-level lists like the existing upgrades.
