Sentinel Concepts

Sentinel is a Base power on the Walls path beside Ward (Tier III, after Alarm). Alarm already outlines intruders near your placed totem and sends you one chat line. Sentinel keeps you watching from anywhere in the world: how many are inside the Alarm cube, who they are, how far from the totem, and at the top level where they are right now. Three ways to show it; every treatment stays off-screen until there is something to show. A player with Thief's Step at a high enough level does not appear.

3. Decisions

  1. Treatment: A Watch Strip (default), B Compass Band, or C Corner Card.
  2. World markers at level 4: on (default) or never.
  3. Where it sits on the tree: Walls path, Tier III beside Ward after Alarm (default), or Tier IV after Ward.
  4. Price: Base prices like Alarm (default), or Raid prices (dearer) to slow the early game.
