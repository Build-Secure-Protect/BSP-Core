Tank Formed Concepts

What a Plasma Tank should look like once the last block completes it. Today nothing changes but the port rings. The brief: the casing on the edges becomes a small outline of the tank and the rest of each casing block becomes glass, so a finished tank reads as finished from across the room. The same 5 x 4 x 5 tank in every view, with two ports and two casing blocks placed on faces (front and top) to show that face casing turns to glass as well. Press FORM to watch the last block go in; drag to turn.

Transition:Fill:

Sweep: a turquoise flash runs from the block that completed the tank along the shell, and each block changes as it passes (about a second). Snap: every block changes at once with one flash on the new frame.

In the mod: the casing already has a formed blockstate. Add six "rail" flags to it (one per direction, true where the tank's edge continues into the neighbour), set by the structure code when the tank forms, and a multipart blockstate draws a glass core plus one rail arm per flag. A face casing gets no flags and shows as glass only. Glass-against-glass face culling is extended to formed casing so the sheet stays seamless. All models and textures come from gen_tank_assets.py. The sweep is a light drawn by the tank's renderer for one second after forming (the blocks themselves switch at once, which the eye reads as the flash passing); Snap needs no renderer work.

3. Decisions

  1. Which look: A Slim Rails (default), B Girder, or C Lit Rails.
  2. Transition: Sweep from the completing block (default) or Snap.
  3. Rail colour: the casing's pale rim grey (default) or the dark hull.
  4. Look C only: the uprights glow up to the plasma level (default, a gauge you can read from outside) or stay fully lit.
