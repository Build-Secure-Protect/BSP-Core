# BSP-Core

**The mod at the heart of the Build Secure Protect modpack.** Build a base, secure it, protect your Shatter Totem, and go and take everyone else's! Every block, machine and screen in here is about the totem: keeping yours, stealing theirs, getting rich enough to upgrade it, or forging a second one. This page runs in the order you meet things: your first minute, the road to a strong totem, the money, the market and the lottery, the plasma, the raids and the forge, your team, and then what the server owner sets up behind it all.

- Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47 or newer, Java 17. Needed on the client and the server.
- Plays well with JEI or EMI (every recipe and machine job), Patchouli (the in-game guide book), Jade (block info), any RF mod (power for the machines), LuckPerms (ranks on kits), and the pack's crushers: Mekanism, Create, Thermal, Ender IO, Railcraft, Integrated Dynamics and Electrodynamics all turn BSP ingots into dust. Tinkers' Construct and Thermal melt and cast BSP metal, never for more than our own machines give, and any mod's pipes and tanks carry the molten metals. The Totem Forge's metal bill asks for Allthemodium ingots by default.
- Made for the other BSP mods: BSP-Teleport, BSP Region Guard, BSP-RankUp, BSP-InventorySync and BSP-Velocity Additions all plug into it. It runs fine without them.

## Your first minute

![The Shatter Totem](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/01_totem.jpg)

The moment you join you get a Shatter Totem, the guide book and 500 Shatter Pieces in the bank. Apart from the Totem Forge at the very end of the road, that is the only way to get a totem. Place it and it projects your base powers in a cube around it. Carry it and it powers you. Keep it somewhere clever, because anyone who finds it can stand beside it for five minutes and walk off with it, powers and all. You get a warning the second they start. There is no power, block or trick that makes a totem safe, and we are never adding one. You cannot hide it in a storage system either: it comes straight back out. If you crash with it in your hands it is placed where you stood, and it is back in your hands when you return, as long as nobody took it in between.

![31 powers on five tiers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/02_totem_powers.jpg)

Thirty-one powers on six paths, bought with Shatter Coins and XP. Carried powers work while the totem is on you, Base powers come from a placed totem, and Raid powers help you take someone else's. Up to eight friends on the ACCESS tab can pass your Alarm and Ward and use your machines, and your whole team can do all of that and upgrade your totem too.

## Shatter Pieces, the bank and the shops

![bank atm](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_bank_atm.jpg)

Pieces are the everyday money, and they are never an item: nobody can steal them. Every player has a bank account, and every day you play, 50 pieces land in it by themselves: at your first login of the day, or at midnight if you are online. Walk up to a Shatter Bank, the ATM the server puts at spawn, and you can pay anyone by name, read your log, and deposit coins. Coins kept in the bank are safe from thieves and stay coins, or you convert them to pieces, a thousand a Copper. A Portable Banker in your pocket does the same anywhere. Once it has unlocked the Shopping Network, every shop flagged for it opens from wherever you stand, and once it has unlocked the market, so does the market. Rent a Safety Deposit Box by the day for the things you cannot afford to lose; a totem is the one thing it will not take.

![shop screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_shop_screen.jpg)

Shops are wherever the server puts them: a Vendor Post with a figure of the server's choosing, a Market Stall in one of eight styles, or a Stall Display floating the wares above it. Every shop is a page of cards: the item, buy in gold, sell dim, and a star for instant buy. Left click buys, right click sells, shift-right sells every full stack. A card can limit how much each player, or everyone together, may buy or sell, and its corner shows what is left: "3/4", or OUT and FULL at zero. Limits come back on their own, after a while, every day, every week or at each rotation, and on a network they count once across every server. Some cards wait for a rank or hours of play and say what they need; a note at the foot of a card tells you what it is for. A card can buy back used machines, energy, upgrades and all, as long as they are whole and hold nothing. Big shops have a search box that looks through every section, or every card of a mod with "@create", and special offers cut prices until a time.

![challenge chat](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_challenge_chat.jpg)

Rotating shops put a few of their cards on show at a time. They can change at a fixed time, every Monday at midnight say, with every card getting its turn before any comes back, and when they change, every player on every server hears what is on offer now.

![land office](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_land_office.jpg)

With BSP Region Guard on the server, the Land Office sells land. Its screen is a map of the ground around you drawn from the terrain: plots for sale in amber, open ground dashed, your own claims in green, the outline you drew with the wand in white. Click a plot for its size and price, then BUY, ENLARGE or GIVE UP. When Region Guard sells land for Shatter Coins, every price reads in coins ("3 gold coins") and the foot of the screen shows the coins you carry: you pay from your inventory and get change.

## Trading and the market

![trade post market](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_trade_post_market.jpg)

The server puts Trade Posts at spawn: a steel trading desk with a screen under a hood, a pair of scales that tips while a trade runs, and four labelled keys, MARKET, SELL, COLLECT and TRADE. The market is one market for the whole network. List up to 9 stacks as one lot for a price in pieces, and it stays up for a week, up to 10 lots at a time; anyone on any server can buy it, and you are paid the moment it sells. Listing costs 5% of the price and buying costs the price plus 5%, and both go into the lottery pot. Lots that run out or that you cancel wait in your Collect box. Search by item or seller, sort by newest, cheapest or dearest, and click a lot to see every stack with its full tooltip before you buy. Unlock the market on your Portable Banker, once, for 12 Netherite Shatter Coins, and you can browse, buy, list and collect from anywhere.

![trade screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_trade_screen.jpg)

Everyone at a Trade Post shows down the side of the screen with the server they are on. Pick a name, press TRADE, and if they accept you both get the trade screen: 12 places and a pieces box each, and their offer changing live in front of you. Any change turns both READY lights off, so nobody gets switched on at the last second; when both are ready a five-second countdown locks it all in. If either of you is short of room nothing moves. Walk away, log out or die and the trade is off, with everything back where it came from. A Shatter Totem and a Totem Core can never be sold or traded, not even in a shulker box.

## The lottery

![lottery page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_lottery_page.jpg)

All that market tax, and half of everything players spend at the shops, goes into one lottery pot for the whole network, and one player takes the lot. There are no tickets: everyone who has played on the network in the past week is in, one equal chance each (staff and operators are left out). The LOTTERY tab on every Trade Post shows the pot, a drum turning through the names of everyone in, your own chance, and the last five winners.

![lottery chat](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_lottery_chat.jpg)

An admin draws it whenever they like. Every server counts down from 5 in chat while the drum spins, then names the winner, and the whole pot goes straight into their bank, even if they are offline (they hear about it when they log in). Then the pot starts filling again.

![lottery banker](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_lottery_banker.jpg)

Your Portable Banker shows the pot, your chance and the last winner on its account page, so you can keep an eye on it from anywhere.

## Plasma across worlds

![plasma accelerator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/render_plasma_accelerator.jpg)

Seven Accelerator Frames round an Accelerator Intake, standing up with the middle empty, and you have a Plasma Accelerator: an octagon with a copper track, eight coil heads and a black sphere turning in a brass cage. Write a Link Card on it, put the card in a Decelerator Core in any other world of the server, and the plasma hops across, powers, access list and all. The port behind the core sends it on to whatever its cables reach, so a Nether outpost runs on an Overworld totem and keeps its chunks loaded through the link. Both ends drink RF; without it nothing moves. Right-click the intake for the Worlds board, one column per world, with every link lit while plasma passes.

![plasma decelerator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/render_plasma_decelerator.jpg)

## The Totem Forge: a second totem

![The Totem Forge: a second totem](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/29_totem_forge.jpg)

The end of the road. Build the 2 by 2 by 3 forge, melt a whole bill of metals into its tanks (128 Tetrium, 32 Magnatite, 8 Charged Magnatite, 12 Illyrium, 32 Allthemodium, 16 Vibranium, 8 Unobtainium and 2 Netherite ingots), fill the mould piece by piece, put the Totem Core on top and press POUR. If you own no totem the pour is free and the new one sets in 5 days of real time; if you do, you pay 4 Illyrium Coins' worth for each totem you own and it takes 15. One pour at a time per player, across the network. When it has set it is yours to take out, and everyone else's to steal: two and a half minutes beside the forge, while you are warned in game and on Discord. Molten Tetrium, Illyrium and Magnatite come in buckets and run through any mod's pipes, and the mould pieces last three pours.

## Herald: your totem calls you on Discord

![herald discord](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_herald_discord.jpg)

Not in the game when someone starts on your totem? Give it Herald, the Base power straight after Alarm, and the BSP Discord bot messages you the moment the game would warn you: the server, the spot, the thief and the seconds left. When it is over you get another: stolen, or saved and why. A thief's Shroud holds the message back exactly as it holds back the in-game warning. Every steal on the network also gets one line in the BSP Discord's alert channel, "A totem is being stolen on Frostwarf", and not a word more.

![herald card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_herald_card.jpg)

Linking takes ten seconds: `/bsp discord link`, or LINK on Herald's card, gives you a six-letter code, and you type `/link` and the code in the BSP Discord. You never type a Discord name or password into the game, and the game never sees your Discord details. If your DMs are closed, nothing is posted anywhere; the game tells you at your next login.

## Teams

![teams screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_teams_screen.jpg)

One team per player, a leader and up to five members, a name and a colour. Teammates have full access to each other's totems: open the panel, buy upgrades and raise the tier (the buyer pays), change chunks, use the plasma machines and projectors, see through the cloak, walk through the Ward and Alarm. Nobody in a team can steal from it, and the compass ignores teammates' totems. The screen opens from the TEAM tab on any totem of yours or a teammate's, from the TEAM button above your inventory, or from a key you bind.

![teams invite](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_teams_invite.jpg)

Inviting takes one click on a name, and the invite lands as a card at the top right with two keys and a minute to answer, a chime, and a clickable line in chat for a player busy in a screen.

![teams messages](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_teams_messages.jpg)

TEAM CHAT sends what you type to the team until you switch back, with a pill above the chat while it is on; `/t` sends one line. Everything said goes on the team's board, so members who were offline read it when they are back, under the leader's pinned note. On a network the team is one team on every server, and the member list says which server a teammate is on. With BSP-Teleport installed there is a TELEPORT key on every online teammate's row.

## Kits

![kits browser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_kits_browser.jpg)

Kits are bundles the server hands out: at your first login, as a reward when you reach a rank, or from the kit browser behind the KITS button above your inventory. Every kit is a card. A ready one has REDEEM on it. One you took lately sits grey with the time until it is ready again. A locked one says why: the rank to reach, with your own step marked on the ladder, the server to be on, the price against your balance, or the slots you need to free. A kit given to you that did not fit, or came while you were away, waits in violet with TAKE IT. Nothing is ever lost: a kit never goes into an inventory that cannot take it, and the price is only charged after that check.

![kits card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_kits_card.jpg)

Click a card's picture to see what is inside, with the counts, and every condition ticked or crossed.

## Scores, seasons and the screens

![score boards](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_score_boards.jpg)

Every totem you own scores by its tier, so stealing and upgrading both move you up. Score Screens at spawn show the live leaderboard, the season's rules and the prizes, and can carry boards from the other BSP mods, such as BSP-Teleport's Home Servers, and turn through all of it, page by page. The Market Board shows the newest lots on the market with their prices, a second after they are listed on any server, and the Lottery board rolls the names of everyone in beside the pot and the last winners. An admin sneaks and right-clicks a screen for its settings: Show it as the scoreboard, the rules, the prizes, one Board, or Rotate through pages; pick the board (‹ and › turn through them when there are more than fit), its look (Ledger rows, Tiles, or a Side column beside the leaderboard), which pages are in the rotation and how many seconds each stays. When a season ends the top three take the prizes, every totem is removed and everyone starts again with a fresh Tier I totem; prizes wait for you if you were offline.

## For server owners

![Vault, screens and admin blocks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/32_base_blocks.jpg)

- **One config file,** `serverconfig/bsp_core-server.toml`: every cost, time, range, limit and price, the bank, the shops, the market, the Totem Forge, teams and kits. The database and Redis passwords live apart in `config/bsp_core-network.toml`, which is never sent to players.
- **Admin Rack,** or `/bsp admin`: every player's totems, position and vault at a glance, teleport and reset, the season end that pays the top three and hands everyone a fresh totem, decoy ranges, chunk loading, and the KITS tab. `/bsp totem wipe <player>` gives a test account a clean slate.
- **Shops are yours to build.** `/bsp shop` opens the Workbench: shops, pages, cards, prices, limits and their refills, rotations, notes, hours, styles and the network flag, all in a screen. A whole price list goes in with `/bsp shop import <file>`: the file is checked from top to bottom first and refused whole on any mistake, `test` shows what it would do, and a price fix keeps everyone's counts. `/bsp shop export` writes shops back out, and `/bsp shop report` shows what every shop paid out and took in, with a spreadsheet of every card. Cards and whole shops can ask for a LuckPerms rank or hours of play. Vendor Posts, Market Stalls, Stall Displays, the Shatter Bank, the Safety Deposit Box, the Land Office and the Trade Post are admin-placed blocks: nobody mines, pushes or blows them up.

![kits admin](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_kits_admin.jpg)
![kits editor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_kits_editor.jpg)

- **The market is yours to tune:** lot size, lots per player, days listed and the tax, items it refuses (ids or `#tags`), and the Portable Banker's unlock price. `/bsp market` lists lots, shows the pot, cancels a lot, ends the overdue ones and opens a player's Collect box. The Totem Forge's bill, pour time, setting times, coin price and steal time are all in the config too.
- **The lottery is yours to run.** The ADMIN key on a Trade Post's LOTTERY tab, or `/bsp lottery admin` anywhere, opens the admin page: everyone seen this season, IN or OUT and why, how many days back a player must have played (7 by default), the LuckPerms ranks to leave out, ADD and TAKE with a reason for the log, and DRAW, which asks twice. `/bsp lottery` shows the same in chat, and `draw`, `add` and `take` do the same from the console. Only one draw runs at a time across the network. The shops' share of each purchase is `shop.lotteryPercent` (50 by default) and the daily login reward is `bank.dailyRewardPieces` (50 by default; 0 turns either off).

![lottery admin](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/feature_lottery_admin.jpg)

- **Kits are made in the panel.** The KITS tab lists them; the editor has the kit's slots, FROM HAND and FROM INVENTORY for enchanted items, an item browser with search and a chip per mod, and every rule as a row of chips: when it is given, whether it is in the browser, once or every so often, the price, the LuckPerms ranks (RankUp's ladder, when RankUp is installed), the servers. TEST ON ME tries it on you; GIVE TO hands it to a player.

![anti totem block](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mrgregles/BSP-Core/main/docs/curseforge/images/render_anti_totem_block.jpg)

- **Anti Totem Block:** keeps totems and chosen machines out of spawn, hubs and arenas, and tells BSP-Teleport where homes and teleports are not allowed.
- **Servers without totem placement:** list the safe servers of the network (spawn, a saferoom, a sanctuary) in `restrictions.noTotemServers`. Totems can be carried there but never placed or dropped, and one carried at logout goes back to its owner's last spot on a server that allows placement, so nobody parks a totem out of reach by logging off somewhere safe.
- **Networks:** MySQL or MariaDB shares totems, limits, the leaderboard, vault totals, seasons, steal warnings, bank accounts, deposit boxes, shops with their stock, limits and rotating cards, the market, the lottery and the daily reward, teams and kits across every server, and Totem Forge pours count once per player; add Redis and all of it is live, live trades between servers included. A kit taken on one server is taken everywhere; a team made on one exists on all.
- **Discord steal warnings:** turn on `discord.enabled` and give each server its `discord.serverName`. The alerts travel over the network's Redis to the Discord bot in BSP-Velocity Additions on the proxy, which alone holds the bot's token: no game server ever has one.
- **The other BSP mods** reach everything through `com.mrgregles.bsp_core.api`: totems, steals, seasons, teams, kits, the bank, the market and trades, zones and transfers between servers. BSP-RankUp hands out kits as rank rewards and its playtime unlocks shop cards, BSP-Teleport moves teammates between servers, Region Guard sells the land, BSP-Velocity Additions carries the chat between servers and runs the Discord bot.
